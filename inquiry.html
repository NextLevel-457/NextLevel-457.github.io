Inquiry


Shadow twists, spirals, folding in on itself, becoming thicker, more distinct.
It forms a shape. It forms me.
I watch from the corner as the human sobs. His windows are closed, as are his curtains. Not a hint of moonlight slips through.
I do this often: observing humans in their despair. And this one seems consumed by his anguish.
I blink my many eyes one at a time. He does not yet notice me, too absorbed in his own sorrow.
His sobs come slower now. He tries to take a breath, but is interrupted as misery consumes him again. He convulses, and begins to cry even harder. Tears fall down his face, soaking into his blankets.
Eventually, he is granted a period of reprieve from his torment. He looks up slowly, and his eyes land on me.
My form is not easy to see amidst the darkness, it is my eyes that give me away. He jumps slightly, but...
He does not scream, nor try to escape. He stares, blinks slowly, then convulses again. His attention turns back to his own anguish.
This is new, something I have not seen before. Humans are curious; they do many things I do not understand. However, without fail, they always fear me. My visage alone is not enough to cause such consistent and powerful fear. It is my very nature to inspire terror, to evoke dread in these odd creatures.
But this one does not react to the fear he must certainly feel. He continues his sobbing, clutching his sides as if they are going to burst.
A curiosity grows within me. What could possibly cause a human to feel such remorse, such powerful anguish, as to overpower my inherent fearfulness?
After a moment, I allow these thoughts to direct my actions. I crawl forth, slowly. My legs, sharp and cruel, carry me towards his bed.
Soon enough, he notices my proximity. Just as before, rather than try to escape, he continues his crying, displaying only a superficial hint of fright.
My curiosity grows. Closer, closer yet I scuttle, keen to discover the source of such pain.
I stand by his bedside. I reach out with one of myriad tentacles. Gently, I brush his cheek.
He jolts upright. He stares at me, tears still falling in great quantities from his face. I blink at him, and join my mind to his.
I feel his fear, but so much more so I feel his grief and shame. He convulses, and the movement pulls his cheek away from my limb.
A moment before the connection breaks, I sense the source of the fresh wave of misery. An image of someone.
His friend turns away from him, but he notices the hurt look on his face. Shame, guilt, and remorse paralyze him. He ran, and now he allows his pain to consume him where no one may observe.
Yet observe I do. I watch him, fascinated by his utter anguish. Surely such a minor incident could not cause this all consuming pain.
A thought occurs to me.
It is a novel idea. I do not interact, only watch. This is my way. I have tried to hurt before, and I succeeded. Seeing a creature's mind broken brought me no joy, and I have not done it since.
Yet this thought grows, as thoughts tend to do. I consider the pitiful form, convulsing and sobbing on the bed before me. I decide that I will do something I have never done before.
Reaching out with my legs, I pull myself onto his bed. In the midst of a fresh convulsion, he fails to react.
Stretching out an appendage once more, I curl a tentacle under his chin and around his cheek.
As the wave of self-hatred subsides, he glances at me. Fear grows in his mind, and he tries to say something. He interrupts himself with a sob as yet another wave of emotion crashes over him.
I begin to delve deep into his mind; past his emotions, through his thoughts, ignoring the pain, shame, guilt, hurt, anguish, and remorse all around me. I look for something else.
I find it. Separated away from the rest, pushed away from the forefront of his consciousness, are comforting memories. I scan them, looking for a common thread.
The way forward on this path I have chosen is clear to me. Pulling out of the depths of his mind, I watch his emotions and wrap another tentacle around his lower torso.
He sobs again, but something glows softly within his mind for a moment. It is drowned out, strangled by the depth of his pain. But for a moment, it was there.
Another tentacle, under his arm and around his neck. I draw him closer, wrap another limb around his chest. His sorrow lessens slightly, shifted aside by a new emotion.
His mind, though consumed by shame, cannot help but wonder at my actions. Another convulsion ripples through his body, but he is held steady by my grip.
His sobbing does not cease, but it does lessen. I continue my task, asserting my hold on him mentally and physically.
He shifts slightly, allowing me to come closer. I sit with him on the bed now, and he allows me to pull him closer, to hold him tighter.
Within his mind, I feel his pain lessen and give way to warmth. Warmth from my cold embrace.
His breathing slows, and his sobbing gives way to mere pained whimpering. His mind calms, and I take this as my opportunity.
I seize his mind, holding it in stasis. He does not think, only feel- unable to prevent my scrying. I look deep into his memories, searching for the real cause of his pain.
I quickly find it. There is a bloody knife. A girl, holding the knife. The blood is her own. She is lying on the ground. She never got up. His own words killed her.
I release his mind, and he cries out, writhing in abject shame and guilt from the memory. I pin his legs to the bed with my own, so that he is held tightly enough by my grasp that he cannot move.
His pain, with no physical outlet, begins an assault on his mind. His anguish suffocates any capacity for rational thought, and he sobs far harder than before.
I wait patiently as he convulses and cries in my grasp, shaking and trembling from his despair. I squeeze him slightly as he tries to take a breath but chokes on his own tears.
Eventually, after much gasping and sobbing, he calms down enough to think. I tentatively attempt communication, unsure how he will respond.
Creature. Calm yourself.
He twitches, and looks up at me. His eyes are red, and his whole face is wet from tears. I gaze at him with my many eyes, waiting for a response.
I send him another thought, watching for his reaction.
What hurts you so?
After a moment, he takes a shaky breath and tries to speak. "I..." He chokes, tears still falling down his face. "I... killed her...!"
His sobs began anew, and I softly take hold of his mind, preventing him from spiraling again.
Creature, how could you be so certain?
"I... I spent so much- so much time-" He gasps for air between words. "time with her... I hurt her the most..."
Another spike of anguish stabs through him, and I do my best to suppress it.
You caused her much joy as well.
He tries to shake his head, but my tentacles still grip him too tight to allow for significant movement. "B-but... at the end..."
You meant no harm by your words.
"She- she still died... because I hurt her. And now- now I hurt him too-" Convulsions wrack his body and he sobs loudly.
I squeeze him tighter, halting his writhing. Your friend will not hold this against you.
"Does- doesn't matter! I hurt him and- and-"
And nothing. This human's pain is ill-justified. Though I feel no concern for him, my urge to free him of his delusions is powerful.
I constrict my hold on his mind so that he does not have the capacity to respond, and can only listen. Fixing my multitude gaze on him, I force my thoughts into his head.
Your friend has been through far worse with you, and is intelligent enough to look past such a simple mistake.
As for her, you had hurt her many times, yet she stayed with you. She returned to you, even when you didn't return to her.
Your offenses against her consisted of no malice aforethought, and the one that you mistakenly believe caused her death was relatively minor.
Tears pour from his eyes, though he does not sob. My hold on his mind is too tight for him to move physically.
Something else happened to her that day, something you had no control over.
You could not have helped, and thus bear none of the blame.
I release his mind, and he says nothing, only continues crying. He trembles, and I pull him closer and hold him tighter.
He cries into me for a long time before finally falling asleep. I uncurl my tentacles from around him and stand up, returning to the corner where I materialized.
The shadows curl around me, and my form melts into them.
