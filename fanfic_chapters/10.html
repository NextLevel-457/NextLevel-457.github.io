"Run- now. Do not allow yourselves to be separated!"
Miku stared at the bridge that Fade had formed, the fathomless void already encroaching upon it's borders. She took a step forward, then hesitated. The ground under her twisted, and she stumbled.
She looked up to see Heliosune racing towards her, hand outstretched, warping space and time under her feet- then a rift opened beneath her, and she fell backwards, clawing at the air, trying to find something to save her. The last thing she saw was Ralsei racing across the bridge, trying to reach her, to rescue her, before the rift swallowed her and left her floating in darkness.
Miku spun around, trying to find something for her mind to hang on to, something to convince her that reality was still a constant truth she could rely on. Then, just like before, motes of light appeared, then grew to fill her vision in every direction. Once again, she was in the space between universes, caught in limbo, this time with no doppelganger to follow.
Vaguely she wondered if one could survive her for long periods of time, for neither air nor food nor water was in abundance here, yet she felt a need for neither. She drifted aimlessly, peering at the various universes, trying to distinguish a way out of the void that surrounded her. No entrance was forthcoming, however, and Miku wondered if she would be consigned to drift here forever.
It would not be accurate to say that time passed here, more so, that time flowed, and rather curiously. Her movements were the only real way to distinguish time, and as they had no rhyme or reason, neither did time. Eventually, a ripple in the surface of one of the orbs caught her eye. As she drifted towards it, wondering what could cause the essence of a universe to be disturbed so, a figure emerged. It paused, perhaps looking around. Miku floated vaguely closer, unsure of their intentions.
Their intent was swiftly made clear, as they zoomed towards Miku with a speed she was unaccustomed to seeing. The figure's form resolved itself quickly, into a lean silhouette with hair closely cropped about their shoulders. Slowing as they got close, they took Miku by the hand, and without hesitation zoomed back to the universe they came from.
This turn of events greatly surprised Miku, but she was altogether too addled, curious, and desperate to resist. As they crossed the threshold of the bubble directly at the epicenter of the ripples, reality coalesced into a much more cohesive whole. Gravity returned to her, and she collapsed onto the ground, gasping for air. When she regained her composure enough to push herself off the ground, her savior extended a hand. Miku gratefully took it, and he pulled her back to a standing position.
They were in a white room with highlights of grey, minimalist walls broken up only by the odd panel, vent, or LCD monitor displaying oddly mesmerizing patterns. "What- where the hell am I now?!?" The figure that had saved her smirked slightly. He was tall, with extremely white hair broken up by streaks of literally glowing neon red. He wore a sharp white uniform with orange accents, and a strange black symbol on the shoulder. "You were out there for far longer than normally advised. Give your mind a moment to regain it's sanity." Miku glared at him. He stared back, displaying more or less no hint of emotion, which only served to annoy her more. "I'll have you know I'm holding my tongue only because I can't find appropriate words to tell you to-" He shushed her with a finger to her lips, which she angrily swatted away.
Before Miku could let him know exactly what she thought of him, he pointed to the corners of the room. "This is being recorded for the archives, by the way." "...hell if I care." she muttered under her breath. "Well then," he pulled out a small piece of paper and deftly unfolded it, producing a pen from his sleeve. "let's begin with the standard examination. Unless, of course, you'd like to plead incoherency." "Can I plead your incoherency?" she snapped.
He chuckled. "Not in this case." Noticing Miku's unamused expression, he tucked away his pen. "Let's try a different approach, hm? Follow me." He walked out a hatch Miku hadn't noticed before, and after hesitating a moment, she followed him.
As they walked, Miku took note of the structure's sterile look and sharp corners, and apparent lack of consideration for comfort. Was this a bunker? Some kind of underground base? Peering down various hallways they passed, she could make out no details that gave her a clue as to their environment.
Walking through another door, she caught a glance of a window in a nearby room. She ran ahead, eager to find out exactly where they were. She stopped dead in front of the window, her breath suddenly escaping her.
A grand spectacle lay before her, as planets and stars revolved around each other in beautiful patterns. Each passing moment they aligned to form a new symbol, each one apparently carrying some significance- she had noticed them scattered around the structure before.
Her rescuer approached her from behind, smiling. "Feeling a little more clear-headed now?" Miku nodded, focused on the celestial view before her.
"Perfect- let's try this again." He extended a hand. "My name is Anxyis(ANKS-yiss), but you can call me Axis." Miku regarded his hand with slight suspicion, then accepted the peace offering. His handshake was gentle, almost jovial in nature. "And you?"
Miku sighed. "I'm Hatsune Miku." He began to roll his eyes, but caught himself. "Well yes, most people here are. You'll have to be more specific than that." She snatched her hand back. "Well, until now, I didn't need to be more specific." She sighed, annoyed with herself for being rude. "Ah- you're new, then. Hm." He took out his pen again and made a note on his paper. She glanced at the pen with interest. "...if you guys are advanced enough to be in space, how come you still use pen and paper?" Axis shrugged. "Higher-ups say it's more cost effective and whatnot. Now, how about you explain what you were doing floating in hyperlimbo?"
Miku shrugged. "Rift? I guess?" He pinched his nose. "Okay. Are you saying a rift randomly formed on your origin plane?" She shook her head. "No, on the Monoplane." He stared at her. "What? I'm not lying!" Axis shook his head. "How could you possibly be naive enough to say your name is Miku, yet also have been on the Monoplane? And under what circumstances would a rift randomly form on the Monoplane?" "It didn't randomly form- Heliosune made it." Axis blinked, then started laughing.
Miku flushed. "H-hey! I'm being serious!" Perhaps a little childishly, she stamped her foot in frustration. "Alright then- who put you up to this? Was it Mynx? They're a liar, I'll have you know- they never paid me back for when I bought them lunch that one time."
"N-no! I don't even know who that is! I was on the Monoplane, and Fade took me to this weird clearing, and then some weird black Miku was there and they were fighting, and then Heliosune showed up and they started fighting, and then there was a chasm, and Fade made a bridge across it but Heliosune opened a rift under me and I got sent here!" Axis promptly stopped laughing. "You what." Miku quickly continued, not wanting to lose his attention. "And I was on the Monoplane cause Heliosune crashed through my dimension right in front of me and my boyfriend, and we went into the rift, and then we tried to follow Heliosune and then met Fade, and- and- oh god, I don't even know where Ralsei is now- and-"
Axis stopped her. "Look- I- wow. Alright" He sat down on a nearby stool. "This- I think this is above my station." He pursed his lips. "Alright then- here's what's gonna happen. I'm gonna have to take you to my commanding officer, and they might take you to their commanding officer, and who knows what happens after that. They're probably gonna have a ton of questions for you, and it's in everyone's best interest that you just answer them, yeah?" Miku blinked, shrinking back a bit. "I- I didn't realize this was such a big deal-" "It's a huge deal! Fade, Heliosune, and Nyx all in one place? Shit probably went down!"
"...oh." Miku shuffled her feet, unsure what to say. Axis sighed. "Look, you're gonna be alright. We have a lot of resources here, we can get you back home, find your boyfriend, whatever. We just need to know what went down so we know how to respond to it, cool? Cool. Come on then." He got up and walked down a nearby hallway, with significantly more urgency than before. Miku watched him leave for a moment, before she processed that she was supposed to follow him and scrambled to catch up.
