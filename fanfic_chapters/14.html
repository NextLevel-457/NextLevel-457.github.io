Heliosune, adjusted to hypertravel from half a century of performing it daily, had learnt the art of landing gracefully from a rift. Every time she landed perfectly on her feet, regardless of the speed and orientation she arrived in.
Ralsei, however, couldn't do any of that. And so he landed on his face.
Heliosune sighed and helped him off the ground. "You're going to have to get better at that." He dusted himself off bashfully. "S-sorry! I'm still, um, really new to this multiversal travel stuff."
She nodded. "It's called hypertravel, for your information. And you'll have plenty of opportunity to get better at landing, because we'll be doing it extremely frequently." She continued without waiting for a response. "Now, you may be wondering what we're doing back at the Monoplane." Indeed, looking around, Ralsei recognized this as the place where Heliosune and Nyx had fought- and where Miku had disappeared in front of his eyes.
"The core of-" "Wait a moment," Ralsei interrupted. She turned towards him, frowning slightly. He stammered a bit, remembering who he was talking to, then continued. "U-um, when- when you and Fade fought Nyx here, and- and she made a bridge across that chasm-"
"You're wondering why I teleported your girlfriend away." He gulped, and nodded. Heliosune sighed in response, and glanced away. "I... underestimated Nyx's cunning. The rift was meant to get her to safety, as I didn't trust Fade's bridge. However, Nyx manipulated the rift somehow- which is extremely rare and very difficult to do- to instead transport her extremely far away.
"Presumably, she was able to do this via the core of the Monoplane's unique capabilities. The energy here may be harnessed to travel very far- it is an in-between place, not meant to harbor visitors for very long. Excepting, of course, Fade- who originates here."
Ralsei nodded slowly. "And now we're going to use it to find Miku." Heliosune shook her head. "No. We're going to use it to catch up with her- the place she was last seen is, as I said, quite far away." She approached the circle of flowers that marked the center of the clearing, and crouched down. Holding her hand out over them, she closed her eyes and focused on the energy emanating from them. She harnessed it, shaped it, directed it through herself and towards her spell- and with a deafening crack, a rift formed above the flowers.
Heliosune stood up, completely unfazed by the noise. "Right. Come on then." She stepped through, and Ralsei followed.
The starry blackness of the rift surrounded him, and the light from behind disappeared as it closed behind them. The stars gradually grew as Ralsei and Heliosune began to accelerate past, speeding faster and faster as the stars grew larger and larger and formed into universes. One directly in front of them depicted a jungle, and far from whizzing past them, they crashed right into it- breaking through the wall and materializing fifty feet in the air.
Ralsei, upon realizing that he was falling, began to flail around for no apparent reason. Heliosune, on the other hand, simply righted herself and waved a hand to slow their fall moments before they collided with the ground. Ralsei, however continued to panic for a few seconds more.
Heliosune raised her eyebrow. "...are you quite finished?" He gradually calmed himself and stood up, shaking slightly. "I- I'm okay. Sorry." She nodded, and surveyed their location, frowning. "Hmm. This isn't where I was aiming for. I hate long distance hypertravel- so imprecise. We'll have to teleport the rest of the way. Come here."
Ralsei shuffled closer to her. She watched him expectantly. "Closer than that." He stepped closer still, so that they were about half an inch away. Heliosune rolled her eyes, and she simply wrapped her arm around his waist, pulled him more or less as close as physically possible, and snapped her fingers.
The world split into a hundred copies of itself, which sped away from each other at incredible speeds before recombining into a cohesive whole once more. Looking around, Heliosune nodded- this was exactly where she originally intended to go. "U-um-"
She glanced down, realizing Ralsei was still pressed extremely close against her chest. "Ah- my apologies. Physical proximity is necessary for communal teleportation." She released him, and he quickly stepped away, blushing furiously and hiding his face in his scarf.
Heliosune promptly strode off in a seemingly arbitrary direction, and Ralsei followed her, albeit at a bit more of a distance than before. "This is the last known location of Anxyis and eight million- I'm sorry, I don't want to say her full designation every single time I have to refer to your girlfriend. Do you have another name I can use for her?"
He thought for a moment. "She... she has a tattoo of the number one on her-" "Excellent. This is the last known location of Anxyis and One, before Anxyis apparently opened a dark fountain and ran off with One- which I highly doubt actually happened.
"The plan is for me to cast a spell that will locate nearby recent rift signatures. We'll follow whatever we find, while I teach you magic that will actually be useful in combat. Once we find Anxyis and One, we figure out what the hell happened and you get sent home with your girlfriend and live happily ever after. Sound good? Good."
As she finished talking, they arrived at the edge of an enclosure. Inside were various trees, and a small Miku with long green hair tending to them. She nearly jumped out of her skin upon seeing Heliosune, who simply waved in her general direction. "Alright then- let's see what we've got." She contorted her hands in various patterns, then snapped her fingers. She didn't move for a few seconds, during which the gardener Miku glanced at Ralsei, who shrugged.
Then all at once Heliosune opened another rift and leaped through it, causing Ralsei to do a double take. He waved at the other Miku, then jumped in after Heliosune.
This time, Ralsei technically landed on his feet- but the velocity they had somehow managed to accumulate resulted in him swiftly landing again, this time on his face. Picking himself up off the ground, he found himself on a stone tower atop a hill, surrounded by a large town. He noticed various brightly colored figures scurrying about amongst the crowds, which he assumed was more Mikus.
"...damn." Heliosune surveyed their surroundings with a scowl. "There's no way we can continue following their rift signatures now- there's about a hundred others scattered everywhere." She stepped up onto the edge of the tower, and held her hand out to Ralsei. He eyed it nervously, but noticing her annoyance, he grabbed it without further hesitation.
Heliosune promptly pulled him up and over the tower wall, flinging him out into empty air. Once again, she saved him a moment before he hit the ground, and once again, he screamed the whole way down anyway. Landing beside him, she wasted no time in striding into the town. Scrambling after her, he glanced around at the crowds nervously. "What's the plan?" She smiled slightly. "When in doubt- employ the masses."
She leaped atop a fountain with ease, and displaying incredible balance, she waved her arms commandingly and began to speak with a force that seemed to reverbate throughout the square. "If I could please have your attention! Almost instantly, the hustle and bustle of the plaza paused as they saw- and heard- the brightly colored, glowing figure perched atop the fountain.
Heliosune spoke again, this time softer. "Wonderful. We are looking for an accomplice of mine- a Miku, like me- who recently passed through here, no more than a day or so before. He would have had white hair with streaks of glowing red, in a sharp white uniform with black shoulder pads. He would also have been accompanied by another Miku, with bright blue hair in two long ponytails- much like mine. If anyone can give us information on where they might have gone, it would be much appreciated. Thank you."
In one final display to fully assert dominance, she performed a double backflip off the fountain and landed next to Ralsei. She calmly seated herself on the edge of the fountain, and gestured for Ralsei to join her. He did, slightly surprised at the casual opulence she had displayed.
"That was... pretty cool." Heliosune nodded. "That's by design." Ralsei shifted in his seat. "...when will you start teaching me magic?"
She thought for a moment. "That depends. This plane's school of magic is relatively minor, not worth learning for your use case. Hopefully the trail will lead somewhere that has a magic system that's both useful and familiar to me. If not, we'll take a quick detour to a nearby plane that has a decent school of magic, get you to cast a few spells, and then you'll have to learn how to crosscast- which just means using magic outside of it's home plane."
Ralsei sighed, and after a moment, Heliosune turned to him. "However- there are some things I can teach you now. You learned magic on Aethra's plane, yes?" He sat up, and nodded excitedly. "Good. Try to cast a simple spell now." He closed his eyes and focused, eager to show off his skills again- but nothing happened. The subtle mental strings he could pull before were no longer there- simply gone.
"You can't do it." It wasn't a question. He opened his eyes and nodded dejectedly. "The magic just- isn't there anymore!" She shook her head. "It is- but not here. It still exists back on Aethra's plane, and you can still use it- it's just harder, because it's further away. Focus hard, now, and look for a shadow- a projection of something that isn't there. That's the magic. Weaker, more distant, but still the same magic. All you have to do is find it."
Ralsei looked closer within himself, searched deep in his mind, but detected nothing. ...those strings... If they were there, if I could pull them- what would I do? Rather than look for something he couldn't find, he decided to just pretend he could find it. He imagined controlling the magic, making it condense water in his hand- and did every part of that, even though it felt like he was the mental equivalent of blind when it came to his magic.
Feeling something, he opened his eyes to see a single drop of water hovering slightly above the palm of his hand. His face broke into a wide smile, while Heliosune regarded it with mild surprise. "...impressive. I suppose that will do as a starting point."
"Ah- excuse me-" She turned to find a tall, brown-haired individual nervously fidgeting behind her. "I, um- I saw the guy you wanted to know about. He was try'n not to stand out, but he was pretty... shiny, right? Anyways, I saw him- I saw him leave out the north gate. I don't know any more than that."
Heliosune nodded her thanks. "Is there anyone watching the walls that I might learn more from?" He bit his lip. "Maybe. The- the guard, they're in a big stone- a big stone building just thataway. One of them might've seen something, I reckon." "Excellent. You're dismissed." The man scampered away, looking rather intimidated.
She watched him for a moment, then turned back to Ralsei. "Anyways. Your magic will no longer operate by the same rules- now, you will be limited by your mana. Mana is a certain energy that your mind has that you use for magic. Run out, and you can't cast magic until it's regenerated. You'll be able to store more mana over time, but for now your stock will be limited.
"Utilizing advanced techniques, it is possible to convert your mana to a liquid that may be stored for later use. There are other important aspects of this, and much more you must learn, but this will suffice as a foundation. Now, shall we inquire as to what the town guard knows?" She smiled, and stood up. Ralsei joined her, and they began to make her way through the crowds in the direction the man had indicated.
