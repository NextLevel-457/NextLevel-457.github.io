Ralsei carefully stepped down the stairs, doing his best not to trip. Though he was grateful to have his old clothes, he couldn't wear them all the time. Aethra had provided a number of other outfits- mostly cloaks the were big enough to function as dresses for him. The one he wore now was a silvery gray, but far from dull, it shone with every color of the rainbow- a magical property, apparently.
Several days had passed since he arrived at Aethra's tower, and since then he had grown significantly more comfortable- but still restless. He hated to be doing nothing, without knowing or doing anything to help Miku. Aethra had attempted to determine Miku's location via magic, but she was unable to divine anything other than the fact that Miku was enjoying relative safety- hardly enough to comfort him.
As he reached the bottom of the stairs, he noticed a distinct lack of Aethra in the room. He quickly found a note by the door, written in glittering lavender ink. It explained that she had left for town to put on a show, and that she would return swiftly. He squinted at the note. Now that he thought of it, it would be odd if his Miku was the only one that performed- but it was hard to imagine Aethra full of energy. Resolving to ask about it later, he glanced around the room. He wasn't hungry- Aethra kept him well fed, and he had woken fairly early. More than food, he often found himself in want of something to do.
Walking over to her caldron, he picked up the enchanted pendant she had created for him. A raincloud quickly formed over his head, and soft, cool raindrops began to fall around him. It was an extremely calming experience, as the water did not linger and dampen anything, but evaporated soon after touching a surface. He breathed in the sweet smell of rain mixed with the ever-present scent of lavender, and laid down on the couch, pulling a blanket over himself. No reason to waste an opportunity to rest- as Aethra always insisted.
As he laid there, the sounds of rain began outside. Lifting up the curtain, he peeked though the window. It was already streaked with raindrops, though not large ones. Aethra preferred this weather over all others, besides a midnight rain. Thinking back, he realized he had not been outside since he had gotten here- perhaps it was time for some exercise.
Begrudgingly, he set down his pendant, stood up, folded the blanket, and walked outside. The sky was a uniform grey, and wind blew softly through the brush. Despite the rain, the ground was firm- dirtying his clothes with mud would not be a concern. Ralsei glanced up at the sky, trying to determine the position of the sun.
After a few minutes, the clouds broke for a moment in the distance, and he noticed a beam of light- that was good enough. He set out in that direction, reasoning that later in the day, the sun would be behind him, and he could follow it back home. Assuming, of course, that he traveled in a relatively straight line.
After trudging in the same direction for a short while, Ralsei crested the top of a hill. The terrain laid out before him for miles, and he settled himself on a nearby rock, deciding to rest and enjoy the beauty of nature. He pulled his cloak tighter around him; the wind was stronger up here, and though he did not mind the cold, freezing to death did not sound very appealing. The clouds refused to part above him, but to his right, he could see a sliver of blue sky- evidence that the shower would be over soon. Surveying that direction a bit more closely, he could also make out a village- or, at least, pillars of smoke he assumed to be a village. The entire area was heavily forested. As he looked out over the landscape, a thought came to him- since his arrival here, he had not seen a single bird. Nor any animal, now that he thought about it. This bothered him more than he expected, as he was not used to such desolate places. The last time he lived somewhere so desolate was...
Was before the Prophecy. Ralsei closed his eyes. His early years, spent alone in a castle entirely too large for one occupant, did not cross his mind often. Very little of note had occurred during that time, and what had occurred he preferred not to- or rather, refused to think about. That warm glow, suffusing his being, flickering across the ground...
No. He forced his eyes open, and stared very intently at the horizon, trying his absolute best to focus on anything other than what had happened then. After a moment, he shed his cloak, and after a moment more, his undershirt as well. The cold wind rushed through his bare fur, chilling him to his bone. He welcomed the cold- it focused his thoughts, brought him back to the present. After remaining like that a few minutes more, he pulled his undershirt back on, donned his cloak, and walked swiftly back to the tower.
When he pushed open the door to the tower, Aethra was there, tending to a fire. Her cheeks were flushed slightly, and Ralsei thought he could hear her puffing slightly. She looked up as he shut the door. "Ah, there you are!" After giving the fire one last good poke, she stood up and walked over to him, hugging him before he could offer a greeting. "Goodness, you're cold! Sit yourself down by the fire, and I'll make you some pancakes. He opened his mouth to thank her, but paused. "...since when did you have a fireplace?" Aethra smiled as she picked out ingredients from a chest near her caldron. "It's always been there, you probably just missed it 'cause of all the curtains."
Ralsei nodded, but sat down at the table, across the room from the fire. "Um, Aethra... Isn't that... isn't that a fire hazard with all the, the curtains around it? ...I just don't want you to burn the tower down by accident..." She shook her head, laughing softly. "It's a magic fire, obviously." She tossed a scrap of cloth into the fire, at which Ralsei flinched slightly. "See?" She deftly took the cloth from the fire and showed it to him. "Completely safe." Indeed, not a thread was blackened by the flame. "A- alright then..." Aethra returned to her caldron, and started measuring out ingredients. Ralsei watched as she held a recipe with one hand, a vial of liquid with another, and used magic to levitate various other pouches and bottles.
"Aethra?" "Mhm?" Ralsei shuffled in his seat slightly. "Can you, um... teach me to- to use magic?" She continued adding ingredients to her caldron, and for a moment, he worried that she hadn't heard him. Just as he opened his mouth to ask again, she sighed and set down her ingredients.
"It's... not that easy." "I- I know!" Ralsei quickly clarified. "I just- I used to be able to use magic, but- but I lost the ability, and- and if it's possible at all to get it back, I want to try." She studied him with her pale magenta eyes and sighed.
"The magic that I'm using, you... you can't learn. It's exclusive to Mikus." Ralsei's face fell, and he slumped back in his chair. Aethra, however, raised a finger. "BUT. This plane does have it's own magic that I can teach you, but I'm afraid it's fairly limited- nothing more than basic thaumaturgy." He nodded, suddenly very focused. "Will I be able to use it elsewhere?" She pursed her lips. "...not unless you become quite skilled. Magic is... complex. Each plane has it's own school of magic, with it's own rules and capabilities. Using magic outside of it's home plane is difficult, but possible. And the rules change." Ralsei stood up. "So what are we waiting for? Can we start now?" Aethra groaned. "...no. Not right now. Because right now, you need to eat pancakes." Ralsei nodded, and proceeded to stuff pancakes in his mouth the second they were placed in front of him. Watching him, Aethra sighed. "...here we go again."
