Ralsei had decided that the Monoplane was a weird place. They had been there for several hours now, lying down and waiting for whatever Fade seemed so intent on witnessing. Those dull hours were enough to convince Ralsei that he wanted to leave as quickly as present circumstances would allow. Thinking rationally, he reasoned that he should be freaking out right now. His worldview had been shattered, and his girlfriend(whom the multiverse apparently revolved around) had pulled him into an extradimensional adventure without his permission. Somehow, all of this didn't bother him. The Monoplane's influence dimmed his emotions, but it didn't impair his thoughts, and his thoughts told him that all of this was absurd. As he laid there, the notion came to him that this was a dream. The thought was comforting, but he knew the reason behind it was weak at best.
He sat up and sighed. Like it or not, this was now the universe he lived in, and there wasn't much to do about it. He got up and walked over to Miku. She sat curled up against a tree, her long, bright teal hair falling around her like wisps of a cloak that they both knew couldn't keep them warm anymore. He sat beside her, and she smiled softly, putting her arm around him. He closed his eyes, and stopped trying to find something to worry about. "...what did you mean, when you said that this was... 'something beyond the veil'?"
She leaned her head on his and sighed. "I... I've always had this... feeling. This hunch. That, the world... that there's more to it than you can see." Ralsei thought for a moment. "What, like a... spiritual world?" She shook her head. "Yes. No. I don't know. Something more, you know? Something that you can't see with just your eyes. Something beyond the veil." Ralsei nodded slowly. "I.. think I know what you mean. And you think this is it?" Miku didn't say anything for a moment. "...it's something, whatever it is." As her grip on him tightened, as if to assure herself he wasn't going anywhere, Ralsei thought about what she had said, trying to make sense of it. He groaned softly. "...I wish..." Miku looked at him quizzically. "You wish what?" "I just wish I could understand, you know? I feel bad that I can't help as much as I want to. But... at the end of the day, I guess we're just- just different people, with separate minds. We can't do much about that." Miku sighed, and nodded.
After a few minutes, Fade stood up suddenly. "They draw near." With this cryptic message, a curious, swirling aura gathered about her hands, and she walked near the center of the clearing. Miku noticed that she was very careful not to touch the flowers. "Who? The... other me that I saw?" Fade shook her head, so slightly as to be almost imperceptible. "Most likely, her quarry." Ralsei stood up, and Miku noticed that he was shaking slightly. "The... the black, armored one? The Knight?" Fade turned towards him, and he felt an impulse to approach her. He did, and she peered into his eyes, searching for something he couldn't see. "You have known... one like her, before. But do not fear. Your adversary is well and truly gone, and you are not of any concern to this foe. Greater forces may be at play here than either of you can imagine, and even if I am wrong, the pieces on the board stand far above you both. I would recommend you keep your distance." Ralsei backed away again, retreating to the edge of the valley where Miku still stood.
"...What did she mean, you've known one like her-" "I don't want to talk about it." Ralsei interrupted, and turned slightly away. They waited, watching Fade begin to hover, pulling more strange particles to her. Miku grabbed Ralsei's hand, feeling a growing sense of uneasiness. "Ralsei?" she whispered. "I really don't like th-" Suddenly, a shadow whipped through the clearing, ripping through reality towards Fade, who's back was turned. Miku tried to cry out, to warn her, but the shadow moved far faster than her voice. However, no intervention was needed, as Fade sidestepped the shadow easily.
She focused her empty gaze on the trees ahead, neglecting to study the direction the shadow came from. Ralsei backed away slightly, in awe of this display of speed and awareness. The clearing was silent for a moment, and the only thing moving was Fade's hands, fidgeting by twisting her fingers strangely. Then, almost as fast as the first, a second shadow raced towards Fade from the direction she was facing. This time, however, Fade stood her ground, and held up her hand that she had been fidgeting with to block the attack. The shadow collided with her, and its form, blurry from its speed, collapsed to a recognizable silhouette- that of the Knight, its blade against Fade's hand.
They remained in that pose for a moment, the Knight's knife in contest against the aura swirling around and through Fade's hand. Just as quickly as she attacked, the Knight suddenly retreated, dashing backwards to strike from a different angle, which Fade met with ease. This time, she pushed back, deflecting the attack and pushing the Knight backwards, where she suddenly sped forwards and struck using her other hand, her speed and decisiveness forcing the Knight back once more.
Miku stood in awe at this contest, no longer sure of her place within the universe that harbored such powerful individuals. For it was clear, by their movements, that each possessed an incredible skill, that neither of them could hope to contest. The Knight dropped their focus for a moment, glancing past Fade, giving her just enough of a window to strike the Knight directly. They flew backward from the force of the blow, but then sped forward just as quickly, dashing around Fade towards the center of the clearing. Fade spun around, but did not give pursuit.
A terrible dread grew in Ralsei as the Knight approached the circle of flowers, but before she reached her destination, a blur of colors sped through the center of the clearing, crashing into the Knight and sending them flying past Fade, who once again sidestepped with ease. The Knight got but a moment of rest flying through the air, before the blur came for them again. This time, rather than a glancing hit, they collided dead on, and the blur came to a sudden stop as their moment transferred to the Knight in a violent, blinding flash. Before them, once more, stood the girl with the visage of a goddess. This time, she did not speed away immediately, but stood still, awaiting the Knight's answer to their assault.
Fade inclined her head slightly. "Heliosune." She nodded back. "Fade. You took a great risk, attacking like that." Miku was struck by her voice- it had the melody and rhythm of song, even with the simplicity and directness of regular speech. Fade cocked her head. "She would not have reached the core- you were there."
Her glowing doppelganger seemed more than mildly annoyed at that. "That was not certain." Fade turned away. "You are always certain- you're the convicted, are you not?" At this, Heliosune bristled, but could not provide a retort before the Knight interrupted their argument. This time, she provided an assault that Heliosune could not so easily answer. They clashed, back and forth, almost faster than the eye could see. Fade watched, uninterested, as their clash shook the trees around them. Ralsei shuddered, and gripped Miku's hand tighter, trying not to stand out amongst the trees they had their back against. Miku, for her part, held him close, but otherwise remained transfixed by the battle taking place. They could see that the Knight's skill outmatched her opponent, and they feared the outcome of this battle. However, as the Knight gained more and more ground, Fade launched a single directed blast into their contest, and the Knight flinched- enough that Heliosune could once again gain the upper hand.
The Knight, sensing her disadvantaged state, backed off, allowing her opponent to launch a counterattack. She fought with the fury of a maelstrom, and the Knight could only block her blows and retreat further. Suddenly, right as she was pushed to the edge of the clearing, the Knight parried an attack- powerfully enough to knock their opponent back, giving them an opportunity to race for the center.
Heliosune cried out, and raced to intercept her, and Fade suddenly leapt up, realizing that something dire was about to happen. Right as the Knight reached the circle of flowers, she stabbed the ground with her knife- Ralsei shrieked, and fell back- and ripped it through the ground, sending a razor-sharp blade of shadow through the ground right in between Ralsei and Miku.
The ground opened up, a shadowy rift that shattered the relative calm of the clearing. Miku, like Ralsei, fell back and scrambled away from the chasm, which widened with a feeling of dreadful inevitability. Fade, acting with a speed and urgency that Miku would not have attributed to her, directed her swirling aura towards the chasm, and wherever it touched, the ground reformed. Fade grabbed Miku, and pointed towards Ralsei. "Run- now. Do not allow yourselves to be separated!"
Heliosune, seeing this, disengaged from her battle with the Knight and raced towards Miku. Fade turned, but before she could stop her, a rift formed underneath Miku, and she fell through before Fade could close it. Ralsei cried out again, and raced across the rapidly dwindling bridge that Fade had formed. The Knight, taking the opportunity, struck Fade from behind. Her knife pierced her aura, and she collapsed. As she did so, apparently defeated, she flicked her hand towards Heliosune, and her light gained a certain sheen, similar to Fade's aura. Without delay, she struck the Knight and began a new furious assault, one that the Knight could not resist. As their form began to flicker, they apparently realized they could not win this encounter. Acting on this realization, they teleported to the other side of the clearing.
Dashing towards her, Heliosune tried to finish the battle decisively, but the Knight vanished before they could, dissolving into shadow. She skidded to a stop, her glow fading. She twitched for a moment, staring at the spot where the Knight had been. "...FUCK!"
