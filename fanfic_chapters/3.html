Ralsei nervously adjusted his tie in the mirror. Taking a deep breath to calm himself, he turned around. "H-how do I look?" Susie studied his suit, rolling her eyes. "I-is it that bad??" Ralsei asked worriedly. "Dude, it's a date, not a funeral. You don't have to wear your butler outfit." "I- I just want to look nice..." Ralsei said, looking down at his outfit. Susie rubbed her chin, then took off his tie and fastened it to his ear. "There. Now you look nice." Ralsei looked in the mirror and flushed. "A-are you sure? I- um..." Susie patted him on the head. "Just be yourself, dumbass. She'll love that." Hearing a honk outside, Ralsei ran to the window and looked outside. "O-oh! My ride's here!" Susie joined him at the window. "She sent you a limo?!? Dude, why are you even worried? Clearly she's already in love." Ralsei, frantically putting on his dress shoes, shook his head. "I- I wouldn't bet on it!" He sped down the steps, nearly tripping in his haste, then stopped at the door to smooth his suit before stepping out into the night.
After thanking the driver profusely, Ralsei entered the restaurant with no small amount of trepidation. This one was even fancier than the one before, and Ralsei felt very much out of his depth despite his suit. He hurried to the table where Miku sat, scanning the menu and seating himself as quickly as he possibly could without looking frantic. "S-sorry to keep you waiting, I-" Miku interrupted him with a smile. "Don't worry about it; you haven't missed anything!" Ralsei nodded, trying to return her smile and only partially succeeding to hide how flustered he was. Miku, seeing his outfit, giggled. "You look so formal-" Ralsei smiled despite his nervousness. "Is- is it too much? I didn't want to- to underdress." She smiled and shook her head. "It's perfect; do you know what you're going to order?" Ralsei quickly scanned the menu and shook his head. "I- I don't eat out much, ha- What do you recommend?" She laughed and tossed her menu into the trash. "No clue: I've never been here before-!" He laughed with her and set his menu down. When the waiter came to take their order, they both simply said, "Surprise me." As they sat and waited for their food, Ralsei racked his brain for something interesting to say, anything at all that would start a conversation instead of just sitting awkwardly. Miku, however, had no such worries, and seemed content to lean back and observe various things in the room, especially Ralsei. "S-so, um, what- do you have any hobbies?" Miku nodded. "A few- Drawing, hacking, learning new languages, soap carving, playing visual novels, disassembling things, weaving, gardening... probably others that I'm forgetting; What about you-?" She smiled at Ralsei, who seemed dumbfounded. Caught off guard that she was now waiting on him to continue the conversation, he flushed and tried desperately to remember what he does in his free time. "O-oh- well, nothing, um nothing impressive like that, but... I really enjoy painting, though I'm not very good at it yet. A-and reading, and just, um, learning new things in general, I guess." Miku nodded. "What kind of books do you like reading?" "W-well, um, usually nonfiction, like, history, a-and recently I've been trying to learn some engineering, b-but it's really hard, so I've also been reinforcing my mathematics." Miku nodded, waiting for him to continue. "...y'know, like... trigonometry. And calculus. All that." Ralsei blinked, waiting for her to say something, but she seemed preoccupied with vaguely smiling in his direction. He waited a few more awkward moments before she started suddenly. "Oh- are you waiting for me to talk? I'm so sorry; I got distracted-" He blinked again. "W- with what?" She smiled, this time displaying genuine happiness. "Don't worry about it." He nodded, and glanced away, trying to discern what she meant. Before he could figure it out, a server arrived with their food. For Ralsei, they had chosen an uni butter pasta; for Miku, an overly fancy lobster. As they began to eat their food Ralsei realized something. I felt... almost comfortable, talking with her. I didn't even stutter- well, not more than usual. He looked over at Miku, who was dissecting her lobster with practiced ease. Taking a deep breath, he decided to stop being nervous and let himself be himself. "Is the lobster good?" Miku nodded with her mouth full of lobster and swallowed. "It's so nice to have a normal conversation for once- I never get to just have a chat with anyone; cause I'm Hatsune Miku, the big star- Miku, the legend. When I try to talk to fans: I end up more lonely half the time, they act as if I'm a goddess and inherently above them-" Ralsei nodded. "I can see that. I'm glad I could provide some comfort." Miku smiled. "It's what I like about you, you know." Ralsei flushed. "Um- you're welcome?" Miku giggled, causing Ralsei to blush deeper. "You try so hard to be nice- more than that, kind. And you don't give me special privileges- you treat me like me." Ralsei looked down and took a second to regain his composure. "W-well- thank you, really. I'm just, um, doing my duty!" Miku nodded. "If only everyone thought that way..."
After they had finished eating, Miku stood up. "This was a lovely night, but- it's about time to head home." Ralsei stood up as well and nodded. "I, uh, I had a good time! Do- um-" Miku put a finger on his lips, smiled, and kissed him on the brow. "Next Saturday, let's go see a movie- how does that sound?" Ralsei, unable to force his braincells to process what just happened, quickly nodded and allowed Miku to lead him back to her car and drive him back to Kris' apartment, where he proceeded to get lost on his way back and Susie had to find him and lead him back to his bed.
