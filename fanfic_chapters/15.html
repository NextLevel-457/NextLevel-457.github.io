Ralsei had been watching Heilosune stare into the distance for what felt like several minutes- though realistically he knew it couldn't have been more than thirty seconds. The last time she had checked for rift signatures, it hadn't taken more then a few seconds. He wondered what could be taking her so long.
In truth, he wondered a lot of things about her. For one, her age- she looked no more than thirty or forty, but she often spoke as if she was far older. Perhaps age worked differently in her plane?
And that's another thing- what plane was she from? He'd never heard her speak of her origin plane, but he was sure she had one. Unless Mikus could just... manifest, which he doubted. At this point, he wasn't willing to put anything past the Mikuverse.
Heliosune interrupted his thoughts with a click of the tongue. "Got it. Only one rift signature is from the time period the guards described, which means that it must be the way that Anxyis went."
Ralsei blinked. "That was... easier than I thought." She smiled slightly. "Easy is a relative term. I have almost a hundred years of experience creating rifts. It's by no means a specialty of mine- there are people far better with far less experience than me- but regardless reading rift signatures has become second nature."
He nodded, then paused. "Wait a second- a hundred years of experience? How old are you?" Heilosune shrugged as she contorted her hands to create yet another rift. "I stopped counting past a hundred and fifty. Age loses its meaning when lifespans exceed a thousand years- anyone older than thirty or fifty is just 'an adult' and that's the end of it."
Ralsei felt his jaw drop. "A THOUSAND? You're gonna live to be a thousand years old??? " She shook her head as she casually ripped a tear in the fabric of spacetime.
"Doubtful- far more likely I'll die by some stupid mistake while chasing after a troublemaker before I'm five hundred. Some people do live that long, or much longer- Fade is older than many universes." Turning away, she added, "For some godforsaken reason." under her breath.
"Now come on- we have work to do." She jumped through the rift, and Ralsei shook his head as he followed, incredulous at this new information.
This time just barely managing to stay on his feet, Ralsei looked around to find himself in a desert. The sun blazed in the sky, a disturbing orange color in stark contrast to the pale bluish-green sky.
"So how on earth do you live that long? Do all Mikus have lifespans that long?? My girlfriend isn't gonna outlive me by a hundred years, is she?" Heliosune rolled her eyes. "Look- magic gives power, and power doesn't die easily. That's one of the most mysterious properties of magic- it passively extends the lifespans of it's practitioners. If One learns enough magic, sure, she could outlive you- or you could outlive her. Or you could both live for hundreds of years, or you both just refuse to die and live forever. Who knows?" She closed her eyes and focused on a spell, leaving Ralsei to ponder this in silence.
After a few minutes, she opened her eyes again, this time with a smile on her face. "Only one rift signature for miles around- we're on the right track. Let's go." She gestured for Ralsei to come closer.
He bit his lip. "Is there another way to traverse long distances without... this part?"
Heliosune sighed and walked over. "Look- if it makes you uncomfortable, I can just pick you up." She did so, sweeping his legs out from under him and catching him in her arms in one motion, holding him close to her chest bridal-style.
Ralsei blushed, but simply nodded. "I guess this- this is better."
She nodded back and closed her eyes. Within a moment's time, they were in a completely different place.
Still the desert, but probably a different place in the desert.
Ralsei looked around. "...did we move at all?"
Heliosune glared at him. "Yes, we did." She set him down, albeit a little less gently than he'd prefer.
Opening another rift, she paused before stepping through. "...This next plane... might be perilous. I suggest sticking close, for your safety. However, it does have a useful magic system, so your training will commence in earnest there."
Ralsei gulped and nodded, following her closely through the rift.
This time, the rift spat them out upside down. Heliosune instinctively spun her body around and landed on her feet, and then caught Ralsei so that they didn't have to add major head trauma to their list of problems. Setting him down beside her(keeping one hand on his shoulder) she glanced around.
They seemed to be in an underground forest. Vines hung down from a moss-covered ceiling, and small shrubs sprouted amongst stalagmites. Light came from curious glowing fruit, granting just enough light to see by.
Heliosune frowned. "Hm. This particular part of this plane is... quite perilous, for the unprepared. Stay very close." She took one of the glowing fruits, and holding it in front of her, began to traverse the uneven ground with far more ease than Ralsei could manage.
He looked around nervously. "We- we're not unprepared, right? ...RIGHT?" Heliosune shushed him. "Quiet. I'd rather not have to fight anything. I assure you, as long as you stay close to me, you're in no danger whatsoever."
Ralsei muttered extremely mild curses under his breath, and did his best to stay close. "...How are you navigating this, exactly?"
"I'm looking for a location where I can use magic. The moss in this plane projects a mild antimagic field, so I can't locate Anxyis or a rift of his precisely without finding a place without it. Stay away from that."
Ralsei had bent down to inspect a mouse with a curiously long tail sitting by a stalagmite. It didn't fear him, but instead gazed up at him with little concern. "Surely this guy isn't a monster?" He giggled as it scampered forth slightly.
Heliosune, with no small amount of annoyance, grabbed him by the scarf and pulled him away. "Gck- hey!" She glared at him again and tossed the fruit she was holding at the mouse.
Quick as a flash, the mouse shot up and wrapped itself around the fruit before being pulled back by the tail into the darkness of a nearby cave. A large SNAP came from where the mouse had disappeared.
"That was a mimic angler. The mouse you saw was it's tongue, and had you tried to touch it, I would be pulling you out of it's stomach by now. Shut up, stay close, and don't touch anything,"
Wordlessly, he obliged.
After far too long walking through the caves- at one point hiding in the darkness from a creature scuttling on the ceiling with far too many limbs- they found a room free of moss and other plants.
Closing her eyes for a moment, Heliosune smiled. "We found them." Ralsei perked up. "They're here? On this plane?"
She opened her eyes and nodded. "That's right. Bad news: they're several miles away, and we can't teleport because of the moss. Which means plenty of time to practice magic."
He slumped a little. She glanced at him, frowning slightly. "Let's start right now. Magic in this plane is used by focusing on what you want to invoke, and keeping any other thoughts from breaking your focus on it. Focus your mana on that thought, and your invocation will materialize. Practice shocking that arachimorph behind you."
"THE FREAKING WHA-" Ralsei was unable to finish his sentence as the arachimorph attacked him, startled by the sudden loud noise. It thwacked him repeatedly with its spindly tentacles as he shrieked.
Heliosune rolled her eyes and magically silenced him, then kicked the creature off of him and watched it scuttle back into the darkness. "What did I tell you about being quiet? "
Ralsei merely trembled on the ground, curled up into a ball.
