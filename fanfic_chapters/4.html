Ralsei smoothed out the wrinkles in his cloak as he stood outside Miku's house. She had invited him over for their fourth date, and Ralsei was simultaneously nervous that he would feel out of place and excited to finally see his girlfriend's house. He noted that while it wasn't exactly a mansion, it certainly stood apart from the neighboring houses. He took a deep breath, and rang the doorbell. Instantly, he heard running behind the door, and Miku threw it open, already beaming with joy. Before he could even greet her, she grabbed his arm and pulled him into a hug. "What took you so long??" Ralsei, with difficultly, pulled himself away enough to be able to speak. "T-this is the time we agreed on..." Miku rolled her eyes, closing the door behind him. "Yes; but obviously, you were supposed to come early because I don't want to wait to see you." He blinked, and opened his mouth to say something, but Miku cut him off. "Well- anyway, come on! I gotta give you the tour." She pulled him further into her house, fast enough that Ralsei had to run to stay on his feet, as she rapidly narrated each room they passed. "That's my kitchen, there's my living room(and my 80" television), that's my study, and that's where I do my virtual concerts, and there's my..." Ralsei's head spun trying to take in all the colors- everything in her house was brightly colored, with posters of herself scattered about in various different artstyles. "Who- who drew those?" Miku skidded to a stop, letting go of Ralsei's hand to pick one up. "These are drawn by my fans- aren't they great? Every day I get so much fan mail; I'm always swapping them out for new ones. Here- let me show you my favorite!" She dashed away and up some stairs, and by the time Ralsei caught up with her, he was gasping for air. Miku giggled. "Sorry for going so fast, I'm just really excited- Come on!" She pushed open the door she stood by, revealing her bedroom. Ralsei walked in slowly, not wanting to disturb anything. However, once inside he realized he wouldn't have disturbed anything even if he was running as fast as Miku was, for the room was big enough that most things were out of reach. Various books were scattered around on multiple desks around the perimeter of the room, with absurd amounts of Miku figurines on shelves dotting the walls in between even more posters of fanart. Above her vivid, bright cyan bed(upon which were multiple impractically large plushies) was a huge poster, beautifully rendered with swirling colors that depicted Miku singing on a large stage, with her music acting as a ladder for a figure who was desperately climbing to escape a churning darkness under them. Behind Miku, dozens of stars dotted an abstract landscape which gradually transitioned to thorns emanating from various silhouettes- some vaguely human, some grotesque, some oddly enticing, and still others merely mundane. The artwork was painted in the spiraling yet vivid logic of dreams, and formed a perfectly cohesive whole despite the conflicting elements. Ralsei could not take his eyes off of it, following the lines and noticing still more patterns within patterns in the work. Miku walked up beside him, smiling as he admired it. "This one was sent to me a long time ago, by someone struggling with more challenges at once than anyone should have to in a lifetime. I've heard from so many who could hardly bear their burdens that were a fraction of what this person had to deal with. And still they persevered. Through all of that, they were able to stay focused on something greater. They wrote me to thank me for my part is supporting them, and I've never forgotten it." She glanced at Ralsei, who was still entranced. "Every time I see it, it's like I'm seeing it for the first time again. I... I still write to them sometimes, to see how they're doing. They're still doing well after everything, and I can't fathom how." Ralsei finally tore his eyes away from the painted to look at Miku. "Wow... I can see why this one's your favorite." She sighed. "So many have collapsed under smaller weights, but they had others to support them through all of it. Inspiring, isn't it? Every day you hear horrible stories of tragedies happening all around the world, and all I can think of when I hear them is what it must be like for the victims. It's nice to hear a success story every once in a while, don't you think?" Ralsei nodded. He turned, and took a deep breath. "Alright. Let's- let's go do something else, otherwise I'm going to want to look at this forever." Miku laughed, and pulled him downstairs, this time slow enough that he could keep up.
"So wait- how does this work again?" Miku rolled her eyes. "It's not that complex, pay attention this time. Whenever I have a concert halfway across the world, instead of flying everywhere, I just put on this suit, connect it to this computer, which manages sending packets to the hologram projector there, rendering the screen here, and making sure all those packets come in in the right order. Now the way it does this is..." Ralsei squinted, trying to follow her explanation but for the fourth time, found himself unable to follow along. "Now obviously that would normally cause a huge delay, but utilizing special networks..." Halfway through her explanation, he simply gave up and started thinking about Starwalker. "But sometimes, if I can't reserve enough bandwidth, I just record it in advance, which means I can't..." Starwalker. Now that guy's original. Wonder if he's ever been to a concert. "Hey! Are you listening?" Ralsei was not listening. "Hm? What?" Miku glared at him for a moment, then giggled. "Alright, alright. How about we do something simpler?" Starwalker was pretty simple. That was a big part of why he's so original. Ralsei started, realizing Miku was talking to him. "Oh- um, yeah! Yeah. Something simple. ...like what?" "Liiikkeeeeeee... stacking giant plushies on top of one another and then trying to climb it and almost dying?" Ralsei stared at her. "A-are you sure about that?" She grabbed his arm and dashed towards her room again. "Yeah, I do it all the time!" When they arrived at her room, Miku released Ralsei's hand and leaped onto her bed. "Ralsei, can you open that closet to your left?" Ralsei went to oblige, but Miku interrupted him as he laid his hand on the doorknob. "Wait- make sure to jump out of the way really fast when you open it!" He paused, confused, then shrugged and opened the door while simultaneously stepping to the side. As he did so, a dozen massive plushies were launched out of the closet, one of them almost hitting Miku. She blinked, startled, then laughed. "I'm gonna need your help stuffing those back in the closet, by the way!" He stared at her, starting to doubt her sanity. "...why do you have so many of these??" "Someone was kind enough to make these, so y'know; gotta make sure they get reimbursed. Now come help me out- these things are heavy;" Ralsei began to make his way over to her, but had trouble squeezing in between the plushies and eventually was forced to give up and clamber over them. Together they undexterously stacked multiple plushies on top of each other. "Alright; do you want to climb first, or should I-?" Ralsei eyed the tower with trepidation. "...what do I do if I fall?" She giggled. "I'll catch you, of course!" He glanced at her, doubting her ability to follow through with that claim. Then she smiled, and he relaxed, smiling back at her. He stared up at the stack of plushies, and began his climb, electing to go as fast as possible to get to the top before it collapsed on him. Leaping onto the tower, he grabbed onto a plush rendition of Miku's hair and started kicking his feet searching for a foothold. Realizing the tower was already starting to lean, he simply pulled himself higher and leaped for the next hold, but landed too hard, pulling the whole tower on top of him. "I got you-" Miku, leaping to catch him a second to late, ended up landing on top of him as he fell onto a plushie that didn't make it on the tower. "Oops." She giggled, as did Ralsei. "W-well- I didn't die!" She laughed at this, wrapping her arms around him. "Thank goodness; am I right?" They laid there together for a few minutes more, each content in the other's embrace, before finally getting up and repeating the process.
Much, much later, after Ralsei finally took his leave and drove home, he collapsed on the couch next to Kris, who was eating popcorn, and Susie, who was eating popcorn kernels. "So how was your date?" Susie asked in between munches. Ralsei laughed. "...just great, Susie. Just great."
