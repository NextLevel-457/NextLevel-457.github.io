Ralsei sat on the gray grass, staring at the spot where Miku had fallen through, into an unknown void. How many universes separated them now? He didn't even know where he was, much less how to get anywhere else. And with such great forces at play here, who would take a moment to help him? He buried his head in his hands, tears forming in his eyes despite the calming force of the Monoplane. He started at the feeling of a hand on his shoulder, and quickly tried to wipe away his tears before standing up.
Fade stood there, and he sniffled slightly, reminded by her form of his girlfriend. She crouched down and wiped away his tears much more successfully than he did. "Peace, young one." He shook his head, unable to find words to express how he felt. She sighed, and took his hand. "Come. Heliosune has agreed to care for you." "W-what about Miku?" His voice caught, fearful of the answer. Fade pulled him forward towards Heliosune, and for a moment, Ralsei feared the worst by her silence. "...the Miku you call lover is far away now- we know not where. But I am sure that wherever she is, she will find Mikus sympathetic to her plight. Many began their story in similar fashion." Ralsei just lowered his head, and let his tears fall, continuing to walk with Fade.
As they reached Heliosune, she turned, and saw Ralsei crying silently. She looked away for a moment, as Fade fixed her empty stare on her. "...it wasn't my fault." Heliosune muttered. Fade said nothing, only continued to look at her. She took Ralsei's hand from Fade, and led him away from the circle of flowers, and into the trees. After they had walked a short distance into the trees, Heliosune put her hand on Ralsei's shoulder. "...I..." She sighed. "...do your best to stifle your despair. Outside of the calm of the Monoplane, your emotions may incapacitate you." Ralsei only nodded, wiping his tears. Her gaze lingered on him a moment longer before she contorted her hands and formed a rift in front of them. Gently pulling Ralsei into it, they allowed the gravity of the portal to take them away.
The rest of the day passed by in a blur, as Ralsei focused entirely on remaining coherent despite the torrent of emotions that raged within him. He barely glanced up at the purplish moon that they traveled beneath, did not marvel at the flowers that sprouted over and through one another to form one beautiful whole. When they reached a tower, standing alone in a field, he did not question it when Heliosune woke a younger, smaller Miku dressed in a long, flowing purple cloak and gently pushed him through the doorway to her, or when she led him up to a room several floors up that was to be his own. He watched from his window as Heliosune created another rift and stepped through it, and finally collapsed on his bed and fell asleep.
That night, he dreamed of swirling hues, whirling chromas, forms that danced and twirled before him, an endless symphony of colors without care or worry, inviting him to join. And join he did, spinning through their dances, revolving and spiraling among these colorful spirits that comforted him at every turn, until his worries and cares melted away. For he reasoned that no matter how pressing they seemed, they could be left for another day. But now, now let him dance and spin and swirl and meld with these beautiful forms.
When he awoke, and remembered all that had happened the day before, he could do naught but cry again. Eventually, he regained his composure, and got out of bed. A change of clothes was waiting for him, draped over the end of his bed. He put them on, barely thinking, completely unfocused on anything until he glanced in the mirror.
His breath caught, for it was a window into the past. His old green cloak fell about his form, with a black heart stitched over his chest. His beloved pink scarf was wrapped around his neck, partially encasing his mouth, and his green wizard's hat shrouded his face in shadow. He had not seen these clothes in years, and it took him a moment to realize that he was wearing them, and that it was not some illusion by the mirror. He looked at himself a bit longer, then wiped away his tears once more and ventured downstairs.
The Miku from the night before was there, stirring a large cauldron. She looked up at him and smiled softly. He didn't return her smile, but merely sat at the table and slumped over. Her smile fell somewhat, but rather than try to comfort him with meaningless words, she simply continued stirring her cauldron. The thought occurred to Ralsei that he should be grateful that she took him in, clothed him, and didn't try to act like his pain could be dissuaded so easily. But as it was, he was consumed by his own problems, and did not say anything.
Eventually, she placed a plate of pancakes in front of him, along with some maple syrup. She sat next to him with her own, noticeably smaller plate. He sighed, staring at his pancakes without moving. The purple Miku poked him gently. "Eat some pancakes." She spoke softly and clearly, without a hint of pity or worry. He sat up straighter, and pulled the plate towards him. He studied his fork, then elected to abandon it and took a pancake in his hand. Taking a bite, he found them to be light and fluffy, their warmth suffusing him. He gradually regained some clarity with each bite, and by the time he had finished, he was thinking clearly enough to take in his surroundings.
The tower was draped with curtains, some dark blue studded with small glowing spots that looked like gems, some a soft lavender, but with a sheen that added many more colors if you looked closely enough. The few windows around the room were blocked by more lavender curtains, but the room remained saturated with light, a soft glow that eliminated shadows and soothed his worries. "Come now, what's your name? I can hardly house you without at least knowing that." He took a deep breath, held it for a moment, then exhaled, forcing himself to relax. "...I'm Ralsei."
She smiled softly again. "I'm Aethra. Don't bother telling me if you need anything, because I'm going to give it to you anyways, alright?" He nodded. Aethra got up, and brought over an armchair, pulling it across the carpeted floor with some difficulty. "Get up- I won't have you mourning in a hard chair like that."
He got up obligingly, and sat down on the chair she provided. It was soft, and gave way enough that the cushions held him like a hug, cradling him gently and keeping him warm. He relaxed, trying to stop being stressed and let the comfort of the room sink in. This became substantially easier when she draped a fluffy lavender blanket over him and set another stack of pancakes on his lap. "Comfortable yet?" Ralsei begrudgingly nodded as his heartbeat slowed for the first time in what felt like days, and his muscles gradually loosened. Aethra performed a curious gesture, waving her hands out over the air with her fingers wrapped around one another, and the air gradually became cooler as the soft pitter-patter of rainfall surrounded him. He closed his eyes, trying not to think about what had happened, or if he was ever going to see Miku again, or Kris and Susie, or if Miku was even alive, or if he was just hallucinating all of this- Something rapped him lightly on the head. "None of that- relax, don't think too hard. Just relax." He blinked, confused. "...can you read my mind?" Aethra rolled her eyes. "Of course not, lunaria," -she said this word with a strange accent, one he couldn't remember ever hearing before- "you're just easy to read. Now relax!" She bonked him on the head again with a curious loaf of bread. He did his best to oblige, leaning his head back to feel the raindrops on his face.
She left him alone like that for several hours, letting him destress in the warm confines of her tower. By the time he noticed her return, his mind was empty- devoid of worries, of concerns. Ralsei sat up and stretched, content, at least for the moment, to relax. Aethra sat down across from him, on a chair he hadn't noticed before. "Well- ready to tell me how you got here?" He blinked. "Wh- what?" She sighed. "I asked, are you ready to tell me how you got here? What's your story? And why are you important enough for Heliosune herself to show up on my doorstep and drop you off here?" He shuffled in his seat, suddenly uncomfortable. She laid her hand on his knee. "Chances are, talking about it will make you feel better. And it'll certainly let me comfort you better." He nodded, and began his tale.
"...and after that, Heliosune took me here. That's all I know."
Aethra nodded. She sighed, and buried her head in her hands. "That's... a lot, isn't it?" Ralsei nodded, trying to maintain his composure. Miku vanishing off to who-knows-where was still hard for him to think about. "...well, if there's any comfort," she said, standing up and moving over to her caldron, "Fade is kind. Well- that might be a bit of an exaggeration-" she added hastily upon seeing Ralsei's dubious expression. "She's- committed to making things right. I doubt she'll leave you like this."
Ralsei tucked his knees to his chest in his armchair. "You think... you think she'll find Miku?" Aethra hummed as she tossed various things into her caldron. "Fade and Heliosune- one might forget you, but not both. Fade remembers little details, but Heliosune is concerned mostly with the big things. Like Nyx." Ralsei glanced over at her. "Nyx...? Is that the one- the one in the armor that I saw?" She nodded, adding various brightly colored powders to her mixture. "She's been a concern for a while now, but- ah, lunaria, I shouldn't worry you with all this."
She rapped her caldron on the side, and a loaf of the curious bread he had seen earlier popped out into her hand. He sighed. "I... I can't believe all this is real. I mean... this is-" "Incredible? Absurd? Unbelievable?" Ralsei blinked. "...well, yes. All of those." Aethra shrugged. "Finding out your entire view of the universe was flawed from the start- and not just yours, but everyone else's, too- that's never been easy for anyone."
She handed him the bread, and he accepted it gratefully. He closed his eyes, finding it impossible not to relax with the taste of the bread in his mouth. Every bite dissolved in his mouth like cotton candy, but exchanged the sickly sweetness for the wholesome warmth and fullness of bread. After finishing his bread, he glanced over at Aethra, who was lounging on a couch by one of the two curtained windows around the room.
"What, um- what was it like for you? When you found out, I mean. ...about all this." Aethra laughed softly. "Well, I already knew there was a multiverse. That's a commonly accepted theme in sorcery, at least on this plane. The fact that I was one of the people that bound it all together... that was a little harder to take in." Ralsei nodded. "...I wonder how Miku is taking it." He slumped backwards in his chair. Aethra pursed her lips. "She'll find her way- I promise. Just... have faith, alright?"
