Ralsei ducked under a branch, careful not to slip into the river beside him. Aethra was somewhere ahead of him, but dense forest combined with the fogginess of the early morning meant that he couldn't see even that short distance. Relying on her footsteps was no good either, as the sound of running water obscured that as well. The only guide he had as to her position was a faint, bobbing light that emanated from a flame she held in her hand to warm her.
Ralsei had denied learning any such pyromancy, and instead focused on basic ice thaumaturgy. He had learned quickly, and was currently capable of a great number of flashy tricks involving ice and snow. More than that, he was teaching himself how to manipulate ice via air currents. Small shards of ice could be propelled very quickly with a strong enough wind; quickly enough to hurt quite a bit. Ralsei had no intention of hurting anyone, however he also refused to be caught off guard in this new and strange multiverse he found himself in.
Distracted as he was by his thoughts, his foot landed on one of the unstable rocks that made up the riverbank. It gave way beneath him, and he barely managed to grab on to a nearby branch that almost broke from the sudden tug of his weight. He carefully pulled himself back to a standing position, resolving to focus on his steps from now on.
The path they were taking was treacherous, and he quietly doubted that whatever Aethra was taking him to see was worth the hike. She had woken him up early this morning, telling him to follow her without explaining where exactly they were going. This was quite unlike her, and Ralsei assumed it was either important or a surprise. So far, it seemed to be the latter, but what surprise would be worth risking falling into dangerous ice-cold water, he did not know.
Soon, the light ahead of him stopped moving. Ralsei eagerly quickened his pace, hoping their destination drew near. He soon caught up to Aethra, and noted with interest that the ground here was much more rocky and flat, as opposed to the dirt trail they had been following which had been dug haphazardly into the side of a hill.
"See how the fog is thicker here?" Aethra's voice betrayed no hint of exertion, despite the taxing journey. "That means it's just up ahead. Stay close to me, alright?" Ralsei nodded, and followed her deeper into the fog. Soon it became thick enough that he could hardly see Aethra just a foot away, and he was grateful for the flat ground. The sound of the rushing river became louder, and freezing water began to lap around his feet.
Within a minute or two, the fog cleared suddenly, as if he had stepped through a curtain. And in front of him was a beautiful waterfall, splashing over rocks above them, throwing shining droplets like diamonds into the air. The whole location was in a large basin, with steep rocky walls surrounding it. Aethra laughed at his awestruck expression. "See now, wasn't this worth the hike?"
Ralsei laughed softly and nodded his head in agreement. Aethra brought out a rippling silver cloth, and laid it out on the shallow water they stood in. Despite the current, it neither drifted away nor absorbed any water, instead floating on the surface. Stepping onto this, she also produced a small wicker basket covered in a more typical lavender cloth, which she set in the center of the platform.
Ralsei sat down on the magical picnic cloth, surprised at how easily it supported his weight. "This place is... incredible." Aethra smiled. "Isn't it amazing?" She set down her flame on the furthest corner of the blanket from Ralsei, a fact which he noted with appreciation.
"This plane has a bunch of dreamlike places like this, but this is one of the most beautiful I've found. I'm quite lucky to live in such a beautiful place." She conjured a blanket for Ralsei, which he took gratefully. He sighed in satisfaction, deciding to be content to enjoy the moment for now.
They sat there a while, enjoying the scenery and Aethra's delicious bread. It was impossible to tell exactly how long they stayed there, as the fog surrounding them scattered the sunlight, causing it to come from all directions. Eventually, Ralsei stood up. "This was lovely, Aethra, but if you don't mind, I'd like to head back home now." Aethra stood up as well, and sighed. "Alright then. Please take time to rest from your studies today, lunaria. Your work ethic is admirable, but no amount of study is going to get you back to Miku any faster."
Ralsei bit his lip. "I... I disagree. If there's any chance that my studies could help Miku, then I won't risk not being prepared. I want to leave to find her soon, and-" Aethra interrupted him. "How, exactly, do you plan to leave?"
Ralsei opened his mouth, then closed it again. "Ah... you... you can't create a rift or something?" Aethra gave him a deadpan look. "You can't. That's the problem. And I can't teach you the magic for it." He stared at her for a moment, then sat back down. "...so I just have to stay here until someone comes back for me? And I can't do anything about it?" She nodded. "I'm sorry, but there's nothing we can do."
Ralsei just shook his head, and tried to smile. "No... no, it's- it's fine. That just means... that just means I get to be here with you, right? It's- it's fine. I'm fine." Aethra said nothing, only sighed and laid a hand on his shoulder. Then, through the fog, a light began to shine.
It grew brighter, bright enough to distinguish multiple colors in it- shades of yellow, red, and blue. Ralsei didn't notice it, but Aethra eyed it suspiciously. "...Ralsei?"
He raised his head to answer just as a blindingly bright figure burst through the fog and landed in front of them. Her light gradually faded, until she glowed only faintly. Heliosune smiled softly. "Thank you, Aethra, for taking care of Ralsei. Unfortunately, I believe that he will not be staying with you much longer." Ralsei leaped up. "What- why- what's going on?"
She raised a finger to quiet him. "Your girlfriend, Miku-8,646,732, recently appeared on a base belonging to the Interversal Normalcy Organization. She studied under another Miku formally known as Anxyis, and just yesterday they visited a nearby planet, ostensibly to practice abjuration. However, they both disappeared. Do you know what was found at their last known location?"
Ralsei couldn't keep the trembling out of his voice. "Wh-what?" Heliosune pursed her lips. "A dark fountain matching Anxyis' soul trait. The fountain was swiftly closed once it was noticed, and Anxyis' current status is unknown, as is your Miku's. I came here chiefly to inform you of this, but also..."
She took a deep breath. "Anxyis, while known to be dissatisfied with his local political climate, has no previous record of misdemeanors whatsoever. I... am hesitant to believe that a youth with a soul trait of honesty would do something so disruptive, and so unexpected. With no recent leads on Nyx, my main target, investigating this has become my priority. And, though I am hesitant to allow this..."
Aethra inhaled sharply. "You want him to go with you." Heliosune nodded. "That is what he wants, is it not?" Ralsei nodded frantically. "Yes- yes! I want to go!" Aethra shook her head in disbelief. "H-how could that possibly be safe for him?!? The places you would be going- it's too dangerous!" Heliosune quelled her protests with a glance, then turned back to Ralsei.
"Have you learned any magic?" He nodded again, and, conjuring some water, began to cool it into a shard of ice. Once he was finished, he tossed it up and propelled it as hard as he could into a nearby tree. It shattered, but not before leaving a deep hole in the bark of the tree. Heliosune observed this with a raised eyebrow. "Any... protective magic?"
He bit his lip and looked down, which she took as a no. Turning to Aethra, she sighed. "He isn't prepared for this- you are correct. However, I very much am. I can teach him along the way, and I have no doubt that he will succeed, if he puts even a fraction as much work into learning basic spells as he did into... that." She gestured towards the tree he had attacked impractically and ineffectively. Aethra said nothing, only glanced away. Heliosune stepped closer. "I understand your concern, but..." She looked back at Ralsei. "He cares for his girlfriend very deeply, and he would sooner try to help and fail than not help at all. This is his crusade- please do not hold this against him."
Aethra only stood silently in response, not looking at them. Heliosune bit her lip, then turned to Ralsei again. "Come- it is time to begin." He glanced at Aethra, then nodded. She waved her hand and created a rift, which closed as they stepped through it.
Once they had left, Aethra stood there a few minutes more, staring at the space where the rift closed.
A single tear fell from her eye.
