As they entered the brightly lit restaurant, Susie was struck by it's opulence. Ralsei, meanwhile, was struck by the fact that they were going to dinner. With Miku. Hatsune Miku. If Ralsei wasn't civil, he would have been uttering profanities to express his utter astonishment. Kris wondered if they had moss on the menu. "Damn, this place is fancy..." Susie muttered. "Are they gonna let you expand your reservation to include us?" Miku giggled. "I don't think it will be a problem\~" Indeed, as they reached the counter, the waiter didn't even question the party's presence. Instead, they led them to a seat so quickly Ralsei had trouble keeping up. Miku seated herself gracefully opposite Susie, who leaped onto her chair and immediately started staring at everything is sight, stunned by the elegance of the establishment. Ralsei elbowed her. "Susie! Please make an attempt to be proper!" Miku laughed again. "She's not doing anyone any harm-!" Ralsei blushed furiously and took his seat, looking down. Kris scanned the menu for moss. Susie also scanned the menu, in her case looking for grilled cheeses. "Kris, they have everything here!" "except moss." "Maybe you can ask for some in a salad." Ralsei squinted at Kris. "You'd rather have moss than anything else on the menu?" Miku scratched her chin, looking very serious. "I wonder if you can get some moss n' cheese." She burst out laughing along with Susie, while Ralsei looked incredulous. "Hey- are the grilled cheeses here good?" Susie asked. Miku nodded. "They put dill on it and everything- This restaurant always goes the extra mile; you should see the shrimp tempura." The waiter soon arrived, asking if they had decided. No, they do not have moss. "no moss? fine. ill have a cheeseburger." Susie snickered. "Two grilled cheeses and a strawberry Fanta!" Ralsei hurriedly scanned the menu again, unable to decide. "Uhmmm... I'll, um, I'll have what she's having." He gestured to Miku, who politely requested the shrimp tempura. The waiter scribbled down their orders and hurried back to the kitchen. Miku smiled. "One benefit of being a pop star- you rarely have to wait long for your food." Ralsei glared at Susie, who was eyeing the flowers, then quickly turned back to Miku. "So- um- what's it like being a pop star?" Miku leaned back in her seat. "Well, for starters, it's about as far from a normal life as you can get- Always dancing and singing, with people looking at me like I'm the most important person who ever lived." "try actually being the most important being that ever lived" Kris mumbled, staring off into the distance. Miku, not noticing Kris' murmuring, continued. "It's a little lonely, honestly; I have to take the backdoor out of my concerts so that I don't get swarmed by fans." She sighed. "But I don't want to completely isolate myself from them either- a star is nothing without her fans, after all. Spending time with fans is always so enjoyable\~ when there's not too many of them; I mean." Ralsei nodded, hanging on to her every word, focused to the point where he didn't even notice Susie eating the flowers. Kris eyed Susie, wondering how flowers compared to moss. Eventually, he decided it was worth a shot. Oblivious to Miku's conversation with Ralsei, he snatched a flower before Susie could eat it and took a bite himself. Miku giggled at the sight, and Ralsei slumped onto the table, holding his head in his hands. Right then, a server brought them their food, and Susie refocused her attention on her grilled cheeses.
Later, after they had finished their food and Miku had paid the bill before Ralsei could offer to split it, they stood up to leave. As they walked out of the restaurant, Susie chatting with Kris about whether they could find a restaurant that did serve moss(and whether or not they could start one), Miku tapped Ralsei on the shoulder. He turned, confused, and Miku waited for a moment until Kris and Susie were out of earshot. "This was fun- do you want to do it again sometime?" Ralsei nodded quickly, trying not to let his jaw drop again. "But- maybe... just us this time\~?" Ralsei's eyes widened. "Um- uhh- s-sounds great!" Miku smiled at him and walked out of the restaurant. Ralsei stood there for several minutes more until Susie noticed they left him behind and went back to fetch him.
