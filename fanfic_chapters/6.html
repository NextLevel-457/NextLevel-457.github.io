Ralsei surveyed the skyline, admiring the juxtaposition of the clouds above and the urban landscape below. The whims of the wind high above did not care for human concerns or efforts, and would dance and spin long after they were gone. He took comfort in that something beautiful would persist throughout his existence, and most likely long after. He was broken from his trance by Miku pulling him closer, and he reflected that it was good to be mindful of beautiful things closer to the ground. He laid his head on her shoulder, content for now to rest with her, high above their worries and concerns. "...Thank you for bringing me up here- this view is..." She trailed off, not wanting to reduce the simple joy of the moment to mere words. Ralsei nodded, understanding what she meant. "It's really something, isn't it?" He sighed. "It gets so much better with you here." She laughed, and hugged him tighter. They sat there for a while, reveling in this immutable satisfaction, wonderful in it's simplicity. Suddenly, their peace was broken as a crack like a thunderbolt assaulted their ears, and reality shattered across the street. The view seemed to be fractured like a broken mirror, and the anomaly's center was what seemed to be a tear in the fabric of the very universe. Out of this rend in spacetime came a dark figure, racing through the city like a shadow in the corner of one's vision, one that makes you suspect you're not alone, even though logically you know that nothing is there. Following the entity came another, brighter form, behind which the rift closed. These two moved at incredible speeds, at angles that should not have been possible, as if physics itself acknowledged their superiority. Ralsei leaped up and grabbed Miku's hand, instinctually racing for cover. Before they could reach safety, however, another crack sounded, and reality once more shattered behind them, on the very rooftop they were standing on. They were thrown to the ground by the shockwave, and the shadowy silhouette raced towards the rift. Ralsei looked up at it, and time seemed to slow. The figure turned to look at him, and for a moment, time rewound. Once more he was standing at the edge of a snowdrift, utter darkness before him, muttering to himself to stay calm, as the Roaring Knight floated before him with its terrible knife. Then he was snapped back to reality as the Knight spun, unsheathing the knife Ralsei had hoped to never see again, using it to rend a projectile sent by the other figure in two. Before Ralsei could react, the Knight dashed through the rift, followed at blinding speed by the other entity. However, they stopped right before entering the rift to glance at Miku, and she was shocked to see herself hovering before her. The other Miku wielded a radiant blade without a hilt, pure and brilliant. She stood tall, unafraid, her fingers contorted in strange patterns, flowing colors surrounding her visage. All this Miku witnessed in a split second before they dashed away again, but this time the rift didn't close. Instead, it distorted, twisting horribly as it began to darken. Ralsei, as if in a trance, tried to simultaneously run away and towards the anomaly, and ended up collapsing. Not knowing what to do, but feeling an irresistible compulsion to follow the luminous form that mirrored her shape, she grabbed Ralsei and dashed through the rift.
For a moment that seemed to last an eternity, the cosmos seemed to swirl around her, dancing, teasing, before it all collapsed into a point behind them. Suddenly, they were in a void. An endless void, broken up by motes of light floating around at distances impossible to judge. Before her eyes, the motes began to expand, to grow, transforming into planets. Though as she looked at them closer, she realized they were not planets, but portals. Windows into other universes, a million, a billion, a quadrillion of them expanded all around her. She realized she had let go of Ralsei's hand, but thankfully, he had not drifted away but floated right next to her. He was staring, wide-eyed, at the gallery of parallel universes around them. He turned to her, hardly able to comprehend what was happening. "Miku? What the FRICK is happening?!?" She shook her head, having spotted her doppelganger amongst the portals. Grabbing Ralsei's hand, she sped forward with a thought, the strange logic of the extraplanar space coming naturally to her. As she watched, the distant figure entered a monochrome landscape, using strange hand motions to create an entryway, which rapidly started shrinking once they entered. Miku desperately tried to increase her speed to reach them before the opening closed, and was only just barely able to float through before it closed completely.
Ralsei slowly picked himself off the ground. The grass around him was a light gray, as was the sky, and just about everything else was various shades of gray as well. He looked around, confused. The sun here offered no warmth, only a strange calm. Miku stood next to him, equally confused, looking around for the one she had seen. Ralsei noted the desolation of the landscape; there were almost no distinguishing features of the land around them. Only an endless field sprawling for miles, devoid of color. "Where is she?" Miku muttered, spinning around, trying to see where her quarry had gone. Ralsei rubbed his forehead. "What- I- is that really what you're most concerned about right now?" Miku shook her head. "She- she was right there- I don't understand..." Ralsei sighed. "Neither of us understand any of this. Let's just- try and figure out where we are." He walked forward, and as he did, the world rippled around him. Miku vanished, leaving only a faint silhouette where she was standing just a moment ago, and flowers suddenly bloomed all around him and for miles around. "Wha- Miku?" Her silhouette stepped forward, and she rippled back into existence. She gasped. "Oh my goodness." She dashed forward, and Ralsei had to squint to follow her ghostly form once she disappeared again. He followed, at a slower pace, marveling as the landscape rippled and changed with his every step. Eventually, he caught up to Miku, who stood next to a tree. Unlike what they had seen before, this tree stood alone, with no others surrounding it. Miku stared up at it, trying to figure out why this tree stood alone, unlike every feature of the landscape that they had seen before. As they watched, a mirror image of Miku stepped out from behind the tree. Ralsei gasped, as did Miku when she looked down to see what he was gasping at. This was not the Miku she had followed. Rather than radiant, this one was muted, and had no eyes- only two black voids. Her hair was gray, her skin a lighter gray, and the rest of her followed a similar pattern. She cocked her head, walking towards them. Strangely, neither of them felt any fear at this anomaly standing before them, despite it's unsettling visage. Suddenly, she spoke. "Where did you come from?" She talked with a soft, monotone voice, one that seemed to dull any extreme feelings they might have had. "I- never mind that, have you seen someone who looks like me but really glowy and colorful chasing someone in black armor?" The entity opened her mouth to speak, but stopped before they said anything. She stepped closer to Miku, peering straight into her eyes. "Where did you see this?" Miku stammered, suddenly intimidated. "W-well- they- they just appeared- where I'm from- and- I followed them- and I thought I saw them go here, but-" She cut Miku off. "Then there is no time to waste. Follow me." She walked off, rippling out of existence as Miku and Ralsei hurried to follow. "W- wait- who are you? And where are we?" "And WHAT IS GOING ON?" Ralsei added. "I am four thousand twenty-three, six hundred thirteen. Known to most as Fade. This," she waved her hand around, "is the Monoplane. Unique amongst the other worlds in the Mikuverse for its lack of inhabitants." "The MIKUVERSE?????" Ralsei sputtered. Fade stopped, and the world ceased to ripple but instead solidified around them, revealing the valley they stood in, surrounded by a sparse forest. In the center of the valley was a circle of flowers, outlining a patch of stone in stark contrast to the grass. Fade sat down calmly on the edge of the valley. "If you truly did see what you thought you saw, then they shall soon come here." Miku sat down behind her without questioning her. Ralsei, however remained standing. "Did you say Mikuverse?? What does that even mean? What was that place we came from? And how do we get back to our world?" Fade waved a hand at him to sit down. "All this and more will be answered in due time. For now, rest." She pressed three fingers against her forehead and pinched her nose between her pinky and thumb, apparently trying to concentrate on something. Ralsei glared at her, then slowly sat down next to Miku. "Are you sure we can trust her?" he whispered. Miku shook her head. "I don't know anything about anything. And I'm not sure I ever did." Ralsei sighed. "You're the one that brought us here." She nodded. "...I'm sorry for dragging you into... whatever this is. But..." She took a deep breath. "This is something real. Something... beyond the veil, you know? I don't know what's going on, all I know is that I need to." Ralsei just shook his head and laid down, resolving to discuss this later. For now, he decided, he just needed to process all this new information.
