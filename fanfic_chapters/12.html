Miku tapped her foot impatiently and checked her watch again. It had been just over a week since Axis brought her here, and he was late every single training session they had. No matter how she pleaded, he insisted that they spend at least a week and a half training, so Miku had decided she might as well make the best of that time and learn as much as possible. So far, they had covered intermediate abjuration, the absolute basics of enchanting, some minor evocation, and short-distance hyperblinking.
Yesterday, Axis promised he'd take her to a nearby planet where they harbored corevines so they could practice her abjuration in a real-world scenario. Interested as she was to see a corevine, she would much rather be learning more evocation- Axis, however, insisted it "probably wasn't necessary" despite her continued requests to further her studies in that area.
Miku's thoughts were interrupted by footsteps down the hallway. She looked up to see Axis jogging towards her. She jumped up to scold him for his tardiness, but he held up a hand to quiet her.
"Before you say anything- may I remind you that I never actually agreed to meet at six am. That's, like, twelve whole hours of training you're making me do every day. Did not consent to that." Miku frowned. "Don't care, didn't ask. Now let's go see those corevines." Axis groaned and trotted off towards the rift center, where permanent rifts were harbored for transport. She followed him eagerly.
The planet Axis had chosen for their training today was covered in a beautiful jungle, with curious grey ferns covering the ground, curling around their ankles as they passed. After walking for a few minutes, following a trail marked with small purple LEDs, they arrived at a gate. It was rather whimsical, with colorful vines weaving around mysterious sculptures in the metal. Behind it lay a field of relatively short trees, only about twice her height. They weren't arranged in any apparent pattern, but rather seemed to be scattered around the clearing haphazardly.
"Alrighty then." Axis spent a moment unlocking the gate, then swung it open and walked inside. Miku followed, trying to spot the corevines he had mentioned, but noticing nothing odd.
Axis continued to the center of the enclosure, then spread his hands and spun around to face her. "See anything?" She shook her head. "Literally nothing." He grinned, and Miku jumped as a vine bopped her gently on the back of her head. She whipped around, staring in amazement.
There was indeed a vine there, but it was so perfectly camouflaged that it was nearly impossible to see unless it was moving. It floated in the air like magic, and Miku couldn't discern where it originated. "We plant these guys around the nexuses of planes, cause they're such good sentinels. That's why they're called corevines!" Axis gave a genuine smile as one of the vines curled around his hand. "They're sentient, don't need training, and pack quite a punch- especially if you're not expecting them. Perfect for defending important areas and for practicing abjuration!"
Miku giggled as a vine wrapped playfully around her leg. "Wow- we don't have anything like this on my plane! But- um, can we start training now?" Axis sighed and sat down. "Fine."
"Let's start with basic knowledge. Recite the five schools of Miku arcana and their uses." Miku shooed away the vine on her leg. "Evocation is the manipulation of the elements, and is mostly used in offensive magic. Abjuration is the prevention of harm, and is used in defensive magic. Enchantment is the imbuement of magic into objects, and has many uses. Restoration is the reversal of harm, and is used in healing magic. Conjuration is the creation and destruction of matter, and is mostly useful in industrial applications."
Axis nodded. "And what is the power of spells based on?" "The strength of your soul trait in your mind at that moment, and your familiarity with the relevant schools."
"That's correct. Demonstrate the simplest abjuration spell." Miku placed the tip of her thumb in between her small and ring fingers, and curled her hand into a fist. Fixing Ralsei in her mind, she focused on the buzzing that was easily noticeable now, and pushed it through her fist into a sphere surrounding her. Her view through the force field wobbled a moment, then settled as the spell took effect.
Axis nodded. "And why would you use another spell over this?"
"Inefficient management of casting capability: protects the area around you instead of you yourself, can't focus on a single direction, doesn't effectively guard against anything but protects mediocrely against everything." Axis smiled. "Very good! Now let's see how that knowledge holds up in an actual scenario."
Miku blinked. "What?" Suddenly, a huge amount of force almost overwhelmed her spell. Remembering her lessons, she fell to the ground and shut her eyes, disregarding balance and vision to focus entirely on channeling her mana into the spell. The corevine struck again, harder this time, then harder again, but still Miku held the spell. She quickly switched hand positions between blows, switching to a shielding spell that specifically guarded against brute force. Maintaining the spell became easier, easy enough that she could open her eyes and start focusing her mana on the spots where the vine was about to strike, lessening her difficulty further. She began to ready an even more efficient shield, but just as she thought she could survive the onslaught- multiple vines struck with overwhelming force, destroying her spell and leaving her trembling on the ground.
When she opened her eyes, Axis was standing over her with a resigned smile on his face. "No matter how good you are, another spellcaster will always find a way to break through. You have to know how to fight back." Miku leaped up, her energy returning in a moment. "Are you saying what I think you're saying?" He sighed. "Let's learn evocation."
Many hours later, Miku was finally managing to funnel her attacks in a specific direction- she had almost hit Axis with a haphazard blast of fire at one point. It was clear that she had a lot more to go, but that didn't matter. What mattered was that she was learning.
Axis raised a hand as her blast of frost froze the corevine next to him. "That's enough for today." The corevine shook itself free and retreated back to the safety of the canopy. "You did pretty good, considering you could barely coalesce a flame when we started." Miku nodded, grinning.
He pursed his lips, seeming concerned. "May I ask you something?" She blinked, surprised by his forwardness. "I suppose so."
"...why are you so eager to learn destructive magic? It's... slightly worrying, I must admit." Miku paused. Why did she enjoy it so much? She thought back across her life, searching her memories for a clue as to why this feel so... freeing.
"...I suppose... I've spent most of my life trying to help people. But no matter how hard I worked, there were some problems I couldn't fix. Some problems can't be solved with songs, you know? This, though..." She channeled a small lightning bolt into the side of a tree. "This can solve a lot of problems, if I use it right."
Axis nodded. "That- no, that makes sense. Yeah." He stared off into the distance a moment, then cocked his head. "...do you hear that?"
Miku listened carefully. Very faintly, she could discern a garbled, reverberating static-like sound. It echoed, even in the small clearing. "That, sounds... familiar."
"I can't think where I'd have heard it befo-" A rift tore the clearing in two, the rippling void shattering the serenity. A figure appeared within the rift, it's silhouette impossible to make out amongst the edges of the rift.
The portal closed, and Miku realized exactly where she knew that sound from. Nyx stood in front of her, her armor seemingly absorbing every iota of light that touched it. Axis stepped back in horror.
For a moment, the world seemed to revolve.
The Knight dashed forward and brought her knife down on Axis, who barely had time to cast a shield. Nyx held her knife there for a moment, Axis utilizing every bit of focus he could muster to stop the knife from reaching him, before she turned her gaze towards Miku. Miku stumbled back, and as Nyx advanced, a corevine swooped down to strike her.
A split second before the attack connected, Nyx spun in a blur, and sliced through the vine with astonishing speed. It fell to the ground, it's camouflage fading slightly.
Axis shouted, sending a shard of ice towards Nyx as Miku backed away. The Knight turned back to Miku, neglecting to block his attack. She raised her knife, preparing an attack, not even flinching when the shard of ice bounced off her helmet.
Miku leaped out of the way as a wave of shadow flew past her, crashing through the walls of the clearing. She barely managed to stay on her feet, and spun around to see Nyx raising her knife to strike.
Fumbling with her fingers, Miku managed to raise a shield as the knife raced towards her. The Knight's raw offensive power nearly overwhelmed her, and she allowed Ralsei to fill her mind, consuming her thoughts. She focused every aspect of her being into keeping that shield up against the growing pressure. Remembering what Axis had said, she shifted the fingers on her free hand, barely managing to keep her panic under control. A spark coalesced, then another.
All at once Miku threw herself out of Nyx's reach and directed her entire mental force to sending a bold of electricity towards Nyx. Her knife slashed just above her, severing a single hair from her head. Nyx was pushed back ever so slightly as the bolt struck her, all of Miku's slight capabilities forced into one attack. Nyx's visor morphed, forming a mouth.
And it laughed.
Axis shrieked as the Knight raised her knife to the sky. It pulsed orange, then blue, as Nyx plunged it into the earth.
Darkness flew forth like a fountain, coursing into the sky, filling the enclosure. Axis grabbed Miku and they ran, ran as fast as they could as the Knight watched, floating there unconcerned. Axis formed a slit in the fabric of reality, a new rift. They leaped through, and reality faded around them.
