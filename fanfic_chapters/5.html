Ralsei glanced up at the treetops above. The sky burned orange in the glow of the sunset, casting shafts of light through the myriad branches down into the forest. Ralsei sighed, then looked back down to where Miku was unpacking the tent. They had decided to spend a night out in the forest together, a few weeks after their first date. Miku was excited, saying she had never been camping before. Ralsei was initially surprised by this, but realized it made sense since she was so busy with being a pop star. He walked over to Miku, already setting up the tent. "Need any help?" Miku smiled at him. "Yes please-" He began assembling poles, however, he found the task to be trickier than anticipated, and by the time he had assembled one, Miku was already finished with the rest of them. "Here; let me just-" She took the pole from Ralsei and quicker than he would have thought possible, she finished the rest of the work. Ralsei blinked in surprise as she inspected the tent. "Looks good to me; what do you think?" He mentally shook himself from his stupor and nodded. "Y-yeah, looks great! You, um, you did that really fast..." She nodded, looking proud of herself. "I've been practicing-" She admired her work for a few more moments, then turned to look at the sun, which despite being mostly obstructed by the trees, still painted a striking picture. Ralsei stepped up beside her and took her hand. She put her arm around him and sighed contentedly. "Hey- we should go stargazing later!" "O-oh- yeah! That, um, sounds fun." Miku pulled him closer. "There's a really nice hill nearby; we can go up there- It'll be so romantic!" Ralsei nodded and smiled, then glanced back at the tent. His gaze lingered there for a moment, then he turned back to the sunset and laid his head on Miku's shoulder.
Later that night, as they neared the top of the hill, Miku could barely contain her excitement. "This is going to be so cool; I've never gotten to see the stars in the wilderness before! Are they brighter without light pollution? Can you see more of them-?" Ralsei laughed softly. "You'll see." Before long, they reached their destination, and the treetops parted to reveal the night sky. Miku turned her gaze upward, and gasped. What seemed to be millions upon billions upon trillions of stars filled every corner of the night sky. It seemed as if they outnumbered the grains of sand at the beach, and each and every one was purer and brighter than any she had seen before. She dropped her pack and seated herself on the ground, in awe of this glorious display, far greater than anything she could ever hope to live up to. Ralsei sat down beside her, smiling at her expression. "Have you ever seen anything like this?" she whispered, not wanting to disturb the beauty that lay before her, that she wouldn't insult the celestial bodies that graciously put on this show for her. Ralsei thought for a moment, then nodded. "I've seen prettier." Miku tore her gaze from the sky to stare at him in shock. "You've seen prettier stars than this?!?" Ralsei smiled. "Just one." Miku paused, then gasped again, and beamed at him. She hugged him, and for a moment, Ralsei felt as if all was right in the world- Miku was smiling, and it was because of him. What more could he want? He hugged her back, and there they laid together, admiring the stars.
Once the night's chill began to creep in, Miku and Ralsei reluctantly returned to their campsite. They enjoyed a light dinner together, then brushed their teeth and retired to their tent for the night. As they lay down to sleep, Ralsei stared up at the treetops, visible through a screen in their tent. He sighed, and looked back down at Miku, who, illuminated by the moonlight, looked almost like a specter- intangible, of a realm outside of his own. Her vivid cyan hair, washed pale in the glow of the moon, weaved around her head in patterns it seemed he could hardly comprehend. A singular tear fell from his eye, and he stared at where it fell in wonder. He breathed in, and was surprised to find his breath shaky. Miku glanced at him, and sat up. "...Ralsei? Are you okay?" He turned away from her. "I... I'm fine." Miku scooted closer to him. "Are you sure?" He hesitated, then nodded. "...if you say so." She didn't sound convinced. He waited for her to return to her sleeping bag, but she stayed by his side. Miku laid her hand on his, and he tensed slightly. "...what?" he murmured. She exhaled softly. "I just wanted you to know that I love you. ...This wouldn't be the same without you." He pulled his hand away from hers. She lingered a moment longer, then turned to go back to her sleeping bag. As she was preparing to sleep, a sound, rippling though the silence of the night, reached her ears. "...you would live." She straightened, suddenly tense. "...don't say that." she muttered. Ralsei offered no response. "Don't. Say. That." she said with sudden force. Ralsei sighed, and Miku turned back to him. "Don't you ever say that. You- I wouldn't-" She stopped, and took a deep breath. "You're wrong. I- I love you, alright? I don't- I've never felt like this before. You're special, you're different." He didn't react to her words. "...there's others." She clenched her fist. "No. No no no no NO. I've heard all this before. And you're wrong." Tears were rolling down her face now. "You wanna know something? I'm asexual. And for a long time, I thought I was aroace. A long time. Sure, I experienced what other people described as love: a fast heartbeat, distracted thoughts, sweaty palms... But if there's one thing I've learned, it's that there's always something deeper. And I never felt anything beyond those things. I refused to believe that's all there was to love, because it didn't really feel like it meant anything. But you... you changed something. Something changed when I met you. And it keeps changing, whenever I spend time with you. You make me feel like something actually matters. Like there's purpose to all this. Like. This. Matters." Ralsei was crying too now, and she stood up and made her way over to him, sitting down in front of him. "...is that really true?" She nodded, and Ralsei saw conviction in her eyes. "But... you..." He sighed. "I come from the Dark World. When I was there, I could use magic. I spent sixteen years of my life alone, sixteen years with only my books. I studied, learned to heal, to fix wrongs and cure wounds. When Kris and Susie came along, I guided them on their path. I assisted them, helped them carry their burdens, pulled them off the ground when they made mistakes. But now... Now I just..." He opened his hands, which were clasped together before this moment. For a moment, Miku thought she saw a mote of light in his palm. "Now I can't use magic anymore. I can't heal. I can't fix anyone's problems, because they're all too big, and I'm too small. At least you can help people with your music. You have so many skills, so many talents, compared to me. What can I do?"
"I can't even comfort myself." He buried his head in his hands, and began sobbing. Miku put her arms around him, and he followed suit. He sobbed into her chest, and she laid her head on his. "You changed me, Ralsei. You don't have to take the entirety of the world's problems on your back, okay? You don't need magic to heal, and you don't need to do everything yourself. Look, at the end of the day, we're together. And that changes things. Some things you won't be able to do, and I won't either. And you know what? It's okay. It's okay, because we'll help each other. Like- like the painting. They got through it because they had people to support them. Us together, we'll do things that no one else can do. Impossible things, if you want to. I'm your girlfriend now, and I-" She stopped, and for a moment, the world ceased to exist beyond the tent. Almost too soft to hear, she whispered in his ear. "I won't let you feel like this."
"I know how you feel. I really do. I've gotten so much heartbreaking mail from fans, about everything they've been through, about how I've helped them through it. And some of them-" She stopped again. "For some of them, it didn't matter. I couldn't help them, and- and it consumed them. I won't even let you start down that path, Ralsei. I love you, and I care too much to let you fall into that pit." Now she was sobbing, just like him. "It's bottomless, Ralsei. It doesn't end. You just keep falling." She tightened her grip on him, and they stayed like that for a moment. Then, Ralsei's sobbing eased, and his breathing steadied as he processed all that she had said. "Neither of us are human, Ralsei. Not really. But we have the same limitations... and the same capacity for love. And you taught me that means something." Something blossomed inside Ralsei's chest, not quickly like in the stories, but slowly. Slowly the darkness began to fade away, as he realized he wasn't alone in it.
