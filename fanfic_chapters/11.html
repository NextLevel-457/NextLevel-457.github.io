The rest of the day passed in a blur for Miku. Axis took her to someone with a more impressive uniform than his, to whom Miku explained everything that had happened to her over the past day. She was assured that her troubles would be taken care of, led to a small room with little furnishings, and told to sleep. Sleep she did, and well at that. The day had been exhausting, and she had little desire to continue it without time to collect herself.
Upon waking, Miku lay in bed for far longer than necessary. An aurora of light hung in the air, giving her something to look at while keeping the room dark. It shone in hues of bright teal with accents of red near the edges. Even with her eyes closed, she could see it dancing beneath her eyelids. She let the shimmering glow lull her thoughts into a state of semi-consciousness, enjoying the bliss of not thinking about all that had happened.
A knock at the door startled Miku from her trance. She fumbled for the light switch and hopped out of bed, wondering what reason there could possibly be to bother her. Opening the door, her expression quickly dropped as she saw Axis standing there, tapping his foot impatiently.
"Rise and shine, One!" Miku sighed. "Why are you here?" He grinned. "I'm your tutor! Now put this on and we can start class." He tossed a set of clothes towards her, which she caught with one hand. "Why do I need a tutor? And why must it be you?" Axis rolled his eyes. "One: you got to learn how things work around here, and two: cause I asked to be, that's why. Now hurry up!" She slammed the door in his face and angrily started changing. Something about him pissed her off, but she couldn't quite figure it out. He was just so... unapologetic about himself. Which wasn't a bad thing, she knew, but he didn't have to be so annoying about it.
Miku opened the door once more to find Axis still waiting there. She didn't know what else he would be doing, but it still pissed her off, acting like she was taking an abnormal amount of time to just change her clothes. He smiled at her, despite her scowl. "Alright! Now that you're properly dressed, we can get started." He sauntered down the hallway, not bothering to wait for her. Miku frowned deeper and followed begrudgingly. "What does wearing a uniform have to do with getting tutored?" Axis chuckled. "Nothing. You just look good in a uniform."
Miku flushed slightly. "What- what are you even tutoring me in, anyway?" Axis stopped suddenly, in front of a hatch with neon green lights outlining it. "Magic, obviously." He pushed open the door to reveal a room full of trees, surrounded inexplicably by blue and green stars floating around the space. The ground was covered in real grass, with mossy paved paths winding around the room. "You want to get back to your origin plane, or find your boyfriend? You're gonna want to know some magic."
Miku gazed around in wonder at the room, a stark contrast to the sterile hallways they had been going through. "Wow... wait a second." She turned to look at Axis, but he had somehow managed to get halfway across the room without her noticing. "Hey- wait! Come back!" She jogged her way over to him, as he took a stupid amount of time to sit down on the grass. In fact, it took the entire time that Miku was running to catch up for Axis to just get comfortable, so that once she reached him, he was staring up at her quite endearingly.
She squinted at him. "What- what are you doing?" He blinked slowly. "Nothing." Miku could have sworn that if one were to write down what he said, there would be a ":3" at the end of it. "...I hate you." She sat down with him, making a point to glare at him the whole time. "Anyway, as I was saying- why do I need to learn magic? You said your whole organization could fix all this!"
Axis stroked his chin, nodding, and altogether looking like an absolute fool. "Well, it's like this. If you were some innocent civilian, a hapless bystander, then yeah. We would fix all this for you. However. You-" he jabbed a finger in her general direction- "are the one and only Hatsune Miku of your universe. You are completely capable of fixing all this yourself- so instead we teach you everything you need to know to do just that. That way, when someone else has a bunch of problems, we can tell you to fix it!"
Miku stared at him. "...are you serious?" Axis shrugged. "Not sure. I've never actually seen them get anyone other than me to fix someone's problems."
"So you're telling me that you're gonna make me learn a bunch of magic before helping me find Ralsei and get home just so that the next time this happens, you can get me to do it instead of you?"
Axis smiled. "Now you're getting it!" Miku clenched her fist. "...I am this close to murdering you right now." She took a deep breath, and slowly unclenched her fist. "Fine. How much do I need to learn before I can go find Ralsei?"
"Well, if you really did come all the way from the Monoplane, then you've got a lot of hyperlimbo to traverse. And that means there's a lot of stuff you have to be prepared to deal with- so at the very minimum, the basics of abjuration and hyperblinking. And, of course, enchanting, for which you'll need to know your soul trait." Miku gaped at him. "Wh- how long will that take?!?"
Axis shrugged. "Most people can pick that up in a few weeks, but judging by the way you're glaring at me, you're gonna brute force your way to doing it in half that."
Miku fell backwards onto the grass and groaned. Even just a week was way too long to sit here without knowing where Ralsei is or if he's okay, and she knew he was probably freaking out. Ralsei was many things, and anxious was one of them.
Axis nudged her foot with his. "Relax. Fade and Heliosune were there, so regardless of what happened, Ralsei's fine. Heliosune would never let anything bad happen to an innocent bystander, and they're both extremely powerful. He'll be okay. But right now, the best way to get back to him as soon as possible is by learning what I have to teach you- so I suggest you pay attention."
Miku sat up slowly and sighed, silently acknowledging to herself that what he said was true. "...fine. Start your yapping." He nodded.
"First things first: The magic I'll be teaching you is a special school of magic exclusive to Mikus- only we can use it. There's plenty of other schools of magic- most planes have one, but this one has everything you need."
"Wait- does my home plane have magic?" Axis shrugged. "Probably, but I don't know what your home plane is." Miku pursed her lips. "Umm... Earth?"
Axis sighed. "Okay, that narrows it down." He pulled out a tablet and typed in a few commands. "What's the current date on your origin plane? That should get us down to just a few candidates."
"Uhhh... August 19th, 2025? Or- it's probably August 20th by now." He scanned the screen with interest. "Alright then- and if your boyfriend is Ralsei Deltarune, then that means unless you live in the apocalypse, you're from plane eight million, six hundred forty-six thousand, seven hundred and thirty two. To answer your question then- yes, and no. Your plane doesn't have magic, but any of it's Dark Worlds will." Miku nodded, remembering what Ralsei had told her when they went camping. "So- can you use that magic outside of the Dark World?"
Axis gave her a curious look. "Most schools of magic can be used outside of their origin plane with enough formal practice, yes. Why do you want to know?" Miku smiled. "No reason. Please continue."
He raised his eyebrow, but set down his tablet and continued his lecture. "Your capacity to cast magic relies on a certain trait- everyone's trait is different. Mine is honesty- if I'm lying, I can't cast magic. But it's not black and white- telling a partial lie only weakens my magic. Because I've worked hard to ensure that I don't lie to anyone, my magic is quite powerful- I'm nowhere near as strong the others you've met, though."
Miku nodded with interest. "So what's my trait?" In response, Axis pulled a jewel-studded orb from his pocket. "No clue- but once you learn enchanting, this can tell you. Each trait has a special jewel that only people with that trait can enchant. This ball has every one of those jewels, and is itself enchanted to cause any enchanted jewels to glow. Simple enough. The only problem is- you don't know how to enchant yet! And before you can enchant, you have to learn basic casting. To start, tuck your small and middle finger under your thumb, then wrap your pointer and ring fingers around it. Like a weird fist- you've got it!" He reached over to her best attempt at his instructions and began to shift her fingers around slightly.
"The better you position your fingers, the easier and stronger the magic will be. Now- do you feel a buzzing in any part of your body?" Miku shook her head. "Pay really close attention- if you still don't feel anything, just imagine it, and that should work well enough. Now- focus on that buzzing, real or imaginary, and push it towards your fist. Not through- just get it to bunch up at your fist. Now, when you're ready, punch towards me, and push the buzzing out of your fist as hard as you can as you do so."
Miku followed his instructions, and focused all her mental might onto pushing that imaginary buzzing to her fist. Closing her eyes in concentration, she punched towards him and shoved the buzzing through her fist. "...hey, I felt something!" Miku opened her eyes to see that literally nothing had changed. "Oh. I was kinda hoping you'd explode. ...what was that even meant to do?
Axis laughed. "No chance of me exploding on your first attempt, I'm afraid. That was a simple shockwave pattern- just about the simplest spell you can cast." Miku frowned.
"And I couldn't do it." He shook his head. "No, there was definite magic there- I felt something. Try focusing on an emotion other than exploding me- chances are, trying to explode people isn't your soul trait."
Miku squinted at him. "And what if it is? Do I just have to be evil?" Axis gave her a deadpan look. "Remember when I said your character determined your soul trait, and not the other way around? If you're soul trait is blowing people up, you're already evil. Now try again!"
She sighed, and closed her eyes again. This time, she decided to focus on Ralsei; on getting back to him, making sure he was safe, and going back home together. She imagined the buzzing- was it easier to focus on this time? -and pushed it towards her fist. Fixing Ralsei's face in her mind, she repeated the earlier motion and-
Blasted Axis across the freaking room. "WHOA!" She opened her eyes and leapt up only to be disappointed in the fact that he had only been knocked over. "Oh. Still pretty cool, I guess..."
Axis picked himself off the ground, gasping slightly. "Dude. That knocked the damn wind outta me! Quick- what did you focus on? It probably inspired your soul trait, and if you can figure that out now, magic will be a LOT easier." Miku blinked. "...umm, I just thought of Ralsei- like, getting back to him and stuff."
He grinned. "No way." "...what?"
"Miku, your soul trait might be love."
